## Journey and economic discipline
An existing booking feature is not an additional insight. The operational choice should identify what becomes confirmed before departure: vehicle/job, parts, price/scope, branch, slot, advisor owner and approval before extras. A positive account can demonstrate a reported successful workflow; a negative account can demonstrate a reported failure state. Neither establishes prevalence or cause.

Treat travel modes distinctly. Waiting at workshop typically means one modelled outward+return journey; owner drop-off/pick-up can mean two such journeys. Outward and return must be queried separately. Pickup removes owner journeys but transfers travel and handling cost to the provider; it does not erase system travel. Home-service applicability must follow task competence and equipment, not generic mechanical-service labels.

Queue, repeat-visit and value-of-time parameters may be explicit scenarios. Do not label arbitrary rates as measured behavior. A reported abandoned booking does not prove a second visit later happened. No 'we measured customer round-trip cost' without observed home and trip records.
