# Start here: Auto2000 Digiroom research

**This archive contains substantial public-source research and licensed raw datasets. It does not establish millions of independent Auto2000 customer observations or a proven sales/profit effect.** The largest corpus is auxiliary Indonesian government-app data. Existing Auto2000/Toyota functions change the baseline for several proposed solutions; current unmet need, operational adoption and incremental business value remain unresolved.

Research cutoff: **11 November 2026**. The endpoint is diagnosis, evidence-backed insights and alternatives for your choice. The release selects no winning solution and supplies no Auto2000 implementation, rollout or investment allocation.

**Start with the expanded [readable findings PDF](output/pdf/Auto2000_Research_Findings.pdf).** The [updated master AI context](context/Auto2000_Master_AI_Context_Updated.md) is the extensive handover for continuing the work. The Word reports and original 16-card insight book below preserve the first wave; the PDF and `reports/EXTENSION_SYNTHESIS.md` incorporate the additional tests and corrections.

The main download contains the original licensed IGAR files. The separately named public-host backup omits those two text-bearing CSVs and retains their nontext derivative, because the privacy screen found plausible self-disclosures. See [README_PUBLIC_MIRROR.md](README_PUBLIC_MIRROR.md) for the exact variant distinction. [EXECUTION_RECORD.md](EXECUTION_RECORD.md) records the refinements, interruption and final checks.

The 6,019,662 station-pair distances were calculations from the station inventory. The full matrix was discarded; useful summaries remain. Additional geography tests preserve those inputs and correct the interpretation of boundary joins and proximity. Synthetic insurance and logic examples are explicitly illustrations, not observed transactions, claims or forecasts. No overall sum across these incompatible units is meaningful.

## Findings worth carrying into your decision

The observed finance simulation already works without compulsory income/contact entry. Comparison, branch discovery, certificate retrieval and dated service-cost guidance also exist. That leaves narrower questions about decision usefulness, confirmation, record scope and evidence accepted at appraisal. Existence does not prove successful fulfillment, while one complaint does not prove universal absence.

Toyota/Lexus xEV volume declined 15.98% while total volume declined 13.59%; portfolio mix moved only 0.36 percentage points. This is a descriptive portfolio result, not Auto2000 volume or a Digiroom effect. A capped trade-in top-up, a full future-value floor and insurer recovery are different obligations. A policy or certificate does not by itself fund a future-price promise.

Current app evidence includes favorable, unfavorable, prospective and resolved accounts. Language changes yielded sharply different displayed BYD review cohorts. The auxiliary IGAR label views disagree on 41.35% of aligned rows, with neither being human ground truth. These findings limit sentiment league tables and simplistic counts.
