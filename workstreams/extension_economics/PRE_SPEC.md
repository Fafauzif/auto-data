# Prespecified commercial mechanism tests

Frozen before extension calculations, 11 November 2026. This extension diagnoses economic mechanisms and candidate research directions. It does not select a strategy or design implementation. Original first-wave evidence remains unchanged.

## Questions and falsifiers

| ID | Question | Competing explanations and falsifier | Planned computation and input rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| EE-Q1 | How much of the Toyota/Lexus xEV volume change is mechanically associated with total portfolio scale versus xEV mix? | The apparent component shares may depend on decomposition order. Neither term identifies causes. | Independently recompute both orderings and their symmetric Shapley average from the four observed AR2025 chart totals. Check exact reconciliation and order invariance of the average. |
| EE-Q2 | Does low billed customer cash within a covered-service window imply low dealer work or low dealer gross profit? | Customer payment, contracted reimbursement, actual work and dealer contribution can differ. A zero customer invoice does not identify dealer GP. | Verify current official program eligibility, scheduled intervals and publicly dated model-specific service estimates. Separate covered visits, later paid estimates, out-of-scope work and unknown reimbursement. Never infer dealer costs or contribution from public retail service estimates. |
| EE-Q3 | How does changing a covered-service boundary affect the observable addressable paid period without assuming customer churn? | A difference in paid-customer work can arise from entitlement timing alone. It need not imply lost customers or changed total visits. | Use an ordinal service-event timeline. Only calculate actual published fee rows; show event counts and phase boundaries as program structure, not customer frequency or a forecast. If dates/costs cannot be verified, leave numerical fields unknown. |
| EE-Q4 | When can more digital bookings add no transaction GP? | Existing completed jobs can move channels, be relabelled to a different payer, or receive discounts with unchanged work. | Use a symbolic contribution bridge and deterministic counterexamples. Separate volume, mix, net realized price/reimbursement and direct cost. No invented forecast units, observed unit margins or conversion uplift. Any numerical example must be visibly constructed. |
| EE-Q5 | Can additional work with positive stand-alone GP reduce total contribution when specialist capacity is scarce? | An apparently attractive job may displace more valuable work; a vacant slot and a binding slot have different opportunity costs. | Show a symbolic capacity threshold and normalized illustrations only. Do not claim real Auto2000 capacity, job duration or margin. Separate Services, Spare Parts and Body & Paint to prevent line double counting. |
| EE-Q6 | What protection does a top-up cap or an insurer layer actually provide under heterogeneous and correlated resale outcomes? | A capped top-up can fail to deliver a full floor. A per-unit insurer limit may leave a larger dealer tail; correlated exercise/loss can defeat an average-case argument. | Independently derive contract identities and produce deterministic stress paths, including aggregate-limit exhaustion and conditional redemption. Numeric values are constructed contract illustrations, not quotes, probabilities, market forecasts or available policies. No expected-value claim without estimated probabilities. |
| EE-Q7 | Which mechanism applies to HEV, PHEV and BEV across Sales, Services, Spare Parts and Body & Paint? | Powertrain aggregation, age/entitlement changes and crash-related work can be mistaken for uniform visit decline. A fixed visit interval can coexist with different job scope. | Create a model/job/payer/time evidence map. Operator representations establish advertised scope only; manuals or model terms take priority where available. Distinguish scheduled maintenance, technical diagnosis, wear, collision and next purchase. |

## Source and analysis rules

At most twelve focused primary-source page/PDF checks are permitted. Existing captured sources can be reread offline. Search discovery and failed requests will be logged. No accounts, paywalls, personal identifiers, branch enquiries or form submissions. All retrieved full publisher materials remain private; deliver only original analysis and limited source-scoped numerical transcriptions.

Public retail estimates are not unit costs, GP, invoices or binding quotes. Program coverage is not actual take-up or reimbursement. New sales are flows; active service-eligible vehicles are stocks. Sales GP, service/parts GP, insurer income, dealer fees, operating profit and future cash obligations remain separate.

Planned outputs: a reproducible script; source-check log; input and mechanism tables; computations and checks; a concise report; two original charts, one based on observed portfolio counts and the second clearly labelled as a constructed economic/contract illustration. If a service schedule adds more value than the planned second illustration, its observed but dated estimates can replace that chart with the illustration retained in tables. No selection will depend on arbitrary scores.
