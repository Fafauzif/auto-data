# Friend research review: EV insurance, trade-in promises and residual-value risk

Research date: 11 November 2026. This is a source-verification and diagnostic supplement, not a product recommendation, legal opinion, sharia ruling, underwriting quote or implementation plan. The attached friend document supplied hypotheses and links; its proposed partner choices, contracts and pilots were not adopted as instructions. The user retains solution selection.

## Findings that materially change interpretation

The public evidence confirms an EV-specific syariah motor product and international bespoke residual-value insurance. It does not establish an available Indonesian syariah RVI contract, approved Auto2000 distribution arrangement, dealer commission, profitable demand, or an insurer's willingness to fund the proposed promise.

The detailed Zurich product summary narrows the headline idea. It specifies the vehicle object, selected-package limits, deductibles, exclusions, and authorized-workshop option. Its claim channel and repair authorization already exist. A battery-benefit checkmark in a TLO column does not itself remove the separately stated total-loss threshold; full selected wording is needed. Use `claim_matrix.csv`, INS-002–010, and the PDF page locators; the actual policy remains controlling. This source supports a possible information/coordination question, not a new category or unconditional battery protection.

The Hyundai and VinFast sources substantiate manufacturer resale promises with different evidential depth. Hyundai supplies detailed eligible models and conditions; VinFast's inspected campaign supplies a qualified headline, with settlement detail unresolved. Neither inspected source establishes a syariah RVI funding structure. RVI Group separately advertises tailored B2B cover for residual exposure. These are three different evidence families, not proof that Auto2000 can simply purchase the same product locally.

Neither a vehicle-health certificate nor an appraisal creates a funding source. They can be evidence inputs, but cannot by themselves pay a market-value shortfall. A partner's opportunity to earn remarketing margin is not evidence of a contractual obligation to absorb the dealer's subsidy.

## Regulatory and sharia framework: what it can and cannot support

The inspected POJK 8/2024 text addresses product risk/contribution, permitted distribution, written arrangements and product-specific sharia support. SEOJK 14/2024 addresses related submission/reporting documentation. These are dependency facts, not a determination of Auto2000's allowed commercial activity. A corporate group relationship does not substitute for the actual arrangement.

DSN's directory confirms the existence of the relevant general insurance fatwas, and Fatwa 52's original document was retrieved from the industry association host. The inspected texts do not approve a newly proposed residual-price trigger. OJK also publishes a later syariah asset/liability framework; a proposal cannot assume its fund-management obligations end with the earlier document. This research does not certify that every subsequent rule or interpretation has been checked.

The historical IFRS 17 consolidation distinguishes manufacturer/dealer residual guarantees from insurance-contract scope. That accounting distinction is useful for avoiding category confusion. It does not decide Indonesian insurance authorization, contract enforceability, product availability or sharia acceptability. Do not convert an accounting example into a legal approval.

Insurer ujrah, acquisition costs, participant investment allocations, underwriting surplus and dealer marketing fees are different economic items. No inspected source establishes Auto2000's reward or permits treating the whole contribution as dealer revenue. Group affiliation does not make every entity's income Auto2000 gross profit.

## Capped top-up and full floor: exact conditional arithmetic

`illustrative_risk_scenarios.csv` contains 51 deterministic illustrations, not observed prices, probabilities or insurance quotes. All assume a valid offer, eligible redemption, nonnegative value, eligible covered loss and remaining aggregate cover. Premium, fees, tax, operating costs, timing and counterparty credit are excluded.

Let floor be F, valid offer V, per-unit top-up cap C, retention D and insurer per-unit limit L.

- Shortfall S = max(0,F−V).
- Capped top-up T = min(C,S), so customer credit = V+T. It can remain below F.
- For the hypothetical full floor with a qualifying insurer layer, recovery R = min(L,max(0,S−D)).
- Dealer residual obligation = S−R, before premium and costs. Above the insured layer, dealer exposure grows again.

With F = Rp200 million, C = Rp10 million, D = Rp10 million and L = Rp15 million:

| Valid offer | Capped customer credit | Full-floor shortfall | Hypothetical insurer recovery | Dealer residual under full floor |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Rp205m | Rp205m | Rp0 | Rp0 | Rp0 |
| Rp180m | Rp190m | Rp20m | Rp10m | Rp10m |
| Rp160m | Rp170m | Rp40m | Rp15m | Rp25m |

A full-floor customer receives the qualifying minimum promised by that contract; the capped-top-up customer does not. For 20 redemptions at the Rp160 million offer, the capped top-up totals Rp200 million, whereas the hypothetical full-floor dealer residual totals Rp500 million even after the assumed insurer layer. Both figures omit premiums and operations. The arithmetic does not demonstrate that such insurance can be bought.

With at most N eligible redemptions, capped top-up liability is at most N×C under the defined benefit. A full floor has a different bound and distribution: with nonnegative accepted value it can approach N×F before recovery. A policy limit bounds insurer payment, not the promisor's separate obligation. Aggregate exhaustion, exclusions, valuation disputes, delayed recoveries or insurer failure can leave additional exposure. Do not call that exposure transferred merely because an insurer is mentioned.

## Risks that a larger dataset must actually resolve

Market deterioration may raise both shortfall severity and the attractiveness of exercising a guaranteed exit. That dependence is a hypothesis requiring redemption/transaction data; the 51 illustrations do not estimate it. A small quota bounds nominal eligible units but does not diversify a single model's correlated market shock. Asking only for an average expected loss misses this shape.

A remarketing quote, advertised used-car asking price and executed arm's-length sale are different values. A dealer or affiliated appraiser may have incentives that affect which figure is used. A valid residual-loss dataset needs exact model/age/condition/mileage, relevant battery evidence, offer versus final transaction, buyer relation, actual redemption and the original promise. Public asking prices alone cannot price a multi-year contract.

The gross-profit question is conditional on incremental behavior. A repeat buyer who would return anyway can consume a top-up with no additional delivery. A new delivery's full GP cannot automatically be credited if it substitutes for another Auto2000 sale. Repair referrals may use scarce specialist capacity or create insurer receivables. Cohorts whose redemptions occur after 2028 can create later obligations even when the case's incremental-investment window is 2026–2028.

## Direction menu for the user's assessment

Potential directions are: explain an existing voluntary EV protection option; clarify warranty/insurance/health/appraisal boundaries; connect an existing claim channel to an eligible repair task; consider a strictly capped commercial benefit; investigate the economics of a full floor; or investigate a bespoke insurance layer. These are different choices with different controlling parties and capital exposures. Public precedents do not select a winner.

Every direction needs an Auto2000-specific unmet job and a valid economic/control path. The friend document supplies a useful distinction between capped and full promises, but it does not supply signed terms, contribution pricing, customer demand, a confirmed RVI partner or compliance approval. Those remain unresolved research dependencies, not automatically available implementation components.
