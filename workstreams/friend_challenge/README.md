## Main analytical extensions

- **FC01–FC02, exposure.** Relative awareness can motivate a discovery question, but exposure, consideration, inquiry, completed drive, SPK and delivery are different outcomes. None of the current public data identifies an incremental exposure-to-delivery effect. The friend-specific Rakuten question/base requires its own verification.
- **FC03–FC11, cadence and identification.** Fewer maintenance jobs per vehicle need not change a published visit interval. Jobs due, work per visit, capture, payer and contribution must remain distinct. National BEV sales mix does not establish Auto2000 installed-base exposure or prove customer loss.
- **FC12–FC17, R1/R2.** Current comparison, finance and test-drive previews exist. Situation-specific fit or handoff improvements remain hypotheses. Specialist branch selection exists, while actual model/job competence, accepted capacity and completed work are not measured. A requested slot, staff confirmation and an attended profitable job are distinct.
- **FC18–FC21, R3/M1/M2.** Toyota already represents health-check and certificate capabilities, and the root agent reports a live digital certificate retrieval flow. Therefore M2's unresolved possibility is usable continuity into appraisal, not inventing a certificate. A valid condition report need not raise resale price or overcome product/price constraints. Home-charging guidance cannot override building permission or true affordability.
- **FC22–FC31, insurance and residual value.** Existing EV syariah cover is a baseline, not proof of customer preference or a ready syariah RVI product. Specialist verification narrows the product to stated BEV scope, coverage limits and optional authorized-workshop benefit. Insurer claim decisions and dealer routing are separate. Capped top-up, full floor, manufacturer guarantee and risk indemnity are different promises. No current partner/underwriter acceptance or product-specific approval is inferred.
- **FC32–FC40, endpoint and evidence boundaries.** Onward mobility is conditional. Channel or payer change alone can leave job GP unchanged. Case line margins are not EV transaction economics. Announced system deployment, outlet counts and survey concerns need their own scopes. Evaluator previews and small user tests cannot establish national or causal transaction effects.
