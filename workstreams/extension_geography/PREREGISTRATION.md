# Geography robustness extension: prospective analysis specification

Written before computing the new scenario outcomes. This is a prospective extension specification, not a claim that the study was registered before the first wave. Known first-wave results are 28 Auto2000-name source records, 27 with another mapped site within 5 km, and a material coordinate conflict at Balikpapan. Schema inspection has established that access eligibility is absent and all source status flags are 1. Initial source inspection also revealed that the 2025 BPS table reports nonmissing child-province stocks in Papua, unlike 2023–24. These observations motivated the tests below.

## Question and falsification rule

The claim being challenged is that a generic charging directory offers weak novelty because mapped alternatives are already nearby. Proximity is necessary for that descriptive argument but does not establish that another site is usable. Treat the proximity argument as materially weakened if fewer than 21 of the 28 source anchors retain a candidate within 5 km under the conservative network-and-venue proxy, or if six or more anchors change by at least 2 km when the candidate pool narrows. These are explicit descriptive decision thresholds, not significance tests or thresholds of customer value. Even if the proxy passes, the claim that nearby sites are actually accessible cannot pass because the source has no current access-validation field.

## Planned tests

1. **Candidate eligibility sensitivity.** Compute nearest non-Auto2000 site for all 28 Auto2000-name anchors under five nested or consumer-specific pools: all mapped alternatives; exclude explicit restriction markers; Toyota-oriented restrictions (retain Toyota/Lexus wording but exclude other explicit restrictions); PLN/CPO network proxy excluding restrictions, dealers and unknown operator classes; conservative subset of that proxy with public-facing name-derived venue types. None is labelled verified usable. A confirmed-current-access pool is not identifiable, giving a known-access lower bound of zero rather than a false zero-site estimate.
2. **Coordinate sensitivity.** Repeat the pools with original coordinates, with source-flagged candidate coordinates removed, with the known conflicted Balikpapan anchor omitted, and with five official branch-direction candidates substituted only in a labelled scenario. Report the original and candidate-coordinate results separately. Branch directions are not a charging field survey.
3. **Units and co-location.** Reconcile sites, charger boxes, source reconstructed charger units, connectors and raw records represented. Exact coordinate duplicates have already been consolidated by the source. Do not subtract the 53 co-location flags again. Estimate connected-component site clusters at 20, 50 and 100 metres as sensitivity units, not proven duplicate sites. Compare provincial ranking under site and box units; neither is capacity, uptime or demand.
4. **Boundary, year and quality sensitivity.** Retain the direct 34-code BPS join as a reproduction of the first wave. Add an explicit old-boundary pooling of 92 into 91 and 95/96/97 into 94. Require national passenger-stock conservation. Compute 2022–25 denominator scenarios holding the June 2026 site numerator fixed; do not call resulting changes station growth. Flag the East Java stock discontinuity and missing-child reporting differences. Recompute 2025 ratios after removing source coordinate flags and after using the conservative candidate pool. Rank and ratio changes are descriptive.
5. **Distance bounds.** Use independent great-circle distances only. For candidate distance d, road distance cannot be less than d, assuming the coordinates identify the intended sites. Report fractions whose lower bound already exceeds 1/3/5 km. Do not invent detour factors, travel times, monetary costs, live queues or plug capacity. Source nearest-road outputs refer to a different, unfiltered problem and will not be reused as scenario driving distances.

Create reproducible Python, scenario-level CSV/JSON, row-level nearest-alternative and classification receipts, province-unit/boundary sensitivity tables, and three figures. Hash inputs.
