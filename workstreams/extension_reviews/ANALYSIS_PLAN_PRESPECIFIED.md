# Prespecified second-round review analysis

This is a locally timestamped follow-up analysis plan written before its new calculations and acquisition. It is not an externally registered protocol or a blind preregistration: the AI researcher has already inspected the original210records and knows the earlier counterexamples. No human validation or causal identification is implied. New pages will be marked prospectively selected under this plan; original and newly acquired records remain separate.

## Fixed sources and analysis units

Freeze the original `workstreams/reviews/reviews_derived.csv`, its validation and AI codebook by SHA256. One platformreviewID is one source record, not one independent person or completed transaction. Review dates, rating, app, platform, requested language/storefront, source versions and source-selection method remain attached. Sourcecounters and derived annotations are not extra original records.

Primary follow-up questions address measurement quality relevant to the case rather than ranking brands. Prior coding `self_reported_app_job` means a specific reported app interaction, including failure; it does not establish a completed customer job. Branch, car-product, prospective and unknown accounts do not become app-job successes. The previous targeted second-AI checks are not a representative validation sample.

## New coding and original-evidence integrity

Do not rewrite frozen first-wave210annotations. New records get a separate namespace and sourcecapture IDs. Coding is source-grounded AI interpretation, with the same primary experience statuses and explicit `independently_verified=false`, `human_validation=false`, `reviewer_residence_verified=false`. If an existing reviewID reappears under anotherlocale, retain the capture relation but do not count another original review. Contradictory source statements retain date/model/version uncertainty and their strongest rival explanation.

Before synthesis, check CSV widths, unique source IDs, old/new overlap, allsource joins, dates/ratings, author/fulltext exclusion and denominator arithmetic. Test outputs must state whether findings are corroborated samplecounterexamples, descriptive patterns, classificationsensitivity, unavailable or unresolved. Internal conversion, actual MAU, branchcapture, demand, causal uplift and dealerprofit are not estimated from these public samples.
