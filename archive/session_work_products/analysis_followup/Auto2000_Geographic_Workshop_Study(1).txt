RESEARCH-LED GEOGRAPHIC STUDY



Where could Auto2000 recover paid
workshop jobs?
Four urban investigations, a 15-market context screen and a reproducible public-data evidence pack.

The question is geographic plausibility. This study compares documented workshop locations
with mapped repair/service candidates and modelled road access. It investigates where a paid-job
recovery pilot deserves further evidence, rather than estimating how many customers will switch.

The run combines 131 reconciled Auto2000 directory entries, 37 OtoXpert listings, Overture
automotive-place extracts and 324 grid origins. These are different evidence units and must not be
added into a customer total.

DECISION BRIEF



A map can identify friction; it cannot
establish a recovered job
The defensible result is a set of access comparisons within explicit study windows. The mapped
repair/service pool is a candidate inventory, not a verified set of equivalent Toyota jobs. Differences
below identify questions for a pilot; they do not measure lost customers.

The separately documented OtoXpert + Shop&Drive oil-network scenario gives snap-screened
median paired Auto2000 gaps of about 1.0 minute in Surabaya, 0.3 in Medan and 2.6 in both
Bandung and Palembang. These are smaller than the broad candidate gaps below, and suggest that
the comparator definition changes the practical story. Exact job equivalence remains unverified.

The minute gap changes materially under stricter provider definitions, so its magnitude is not a
stable estimate of competitive disadvantage. The strategy also changes in two ways before any
forecast is justified. First, customer-paid work is not synonymous with an expired vehicle warranty.
Second, economical workshop and parts routes already exist through OtoXpert and T-OPT. Operator
identity matters for whose contribution a recovered job would create.

COHORT CORRECTION



Define the cohort by payer and
entitlement, not age alone
The original plan prioritised out-of-warranty petrol Toyota owners. Official T-CARE XTRA terms
identify customer-paid scheduled services that can contribute to additional warranty eligibility. A
paid job can therefore coexist with warranty coverage.

The practical cohort becomes owners considering a customer-paid job outside relevant free or
prepaid service entitlements. Vehicle age, odometer, prior service history and warranty status are
separate attributes. Public locations cannot identify this cohort at the origin level.

The primary spatial layer remains general petrol-Toyota workshop access. HEV/PHEV and BEV paths
are separate because a general-service listing does not establish local competence for a high-voltage
task.

STUDY DESIGN



Four access investigations; fifteen
markets remain in view
The design combines a national contextual footprint with a purposive 15-market screen and four
bounded urban windows. Surabaya, Medan, Bandung and Palembang offer verified official-directory
evidence, contrasting locations and feasible road-routing comparisons.

This is a comparative case design, not a representative sample of Indonesian motorists. The
rectangles are analytical windows, not administrative metro boundaries. Their finite destination pools
extend approximately 0.08 degrees beyond each origin window.

Jakarta and other markets remain in the screening dataset. Their exclusion from detailed routing is a
scope decision, not evidence of low opportunity. Data availability is documented separately from
attractiveness.

Selection is transparent, but it is not a

EVIDENCE ARCHITECTURE



Three location inventories answer
different questions
The Auto2000 workstream observed 155 directory cards and reconciled them to 131 unique
name/coordinate entries. Repeated city-directory content does not establish additional branches.
Some listings are showroom-only or body-and-paint locations.

The OtoXpert directory returned 37 listings with operator labels. The automotive-place workstream
contains 45,202 extracted rows, of which 3,359 pass its candidate filters. Confidence scores,
category labels and names do not verify Toyota fitment or a specific job.

The archived SPKLU inventory adds 2,454 charging-site rows from June 2026. It answers a
charging-context question and is not a branch inventory or current operation register.

SERVICE EVIDENCE



Equivalent jobs remain a verification
task
OtoXpert advertises an oil/gasket/filter package from IDR299,000 and an approximate 30-minute
service. That is a network-level advertised starting offer, not a current quote for the selected Toyota
variant or an available slot.

Toyota also advertises brake work through OtoXpert and oil-filter/brake-pad categories in T-OPT.
These sources justify testing existing economical routes. They do not establish actual outlet stock,
fitment, installed price or service capacity.

No matched, model-specific branch and independent-garage quotes were obtained in this public-data
run. Accordingly, the report makes no measured dealer-price-premium claim and does not present
the candidate road-access comparison as a like-for-like job choice.

OIL-SERVICE EVIDENCE / CLASSIFICATION COUNTEREXAMPLE



A parts-store category can hide a
documented service option
The generic automotive filter excluded some Shop&Drive records because the source classified them
as auto-parts stores. Official issuer information nevertheless advertises oil and oil-filter services and
names Toyota among supported brands. Category-only discovery can therefore miss relevant service
pathways.

The official Shop&Drive directory supplied 425 structured location records. Supplementary routing
adds 5,913 pairs to the main 76,221, totalling 82,134 model comparisons. Of the 425 official
Shop&Drive listings, 67 fall within the four expanded deep-dive rectangles: 28 Surabaya, 8 Medan,
22 Bandung and 9 Palembang. These are published coordinate candidates, not field-validated
entrances or a claim about current workshop capacity.

This prompted a disclosed supplementary analysis after the main candidate rules were frozen. The
supplement improves service relevance but does not prove the exact current Toyota model/year, oil
specification, filter fitment, stock or appointment.

ORIGIN DESIGN



The denominator is a grid, not a
customer base
The main routing run uses 324 regular grid-cell centres across four windows. Each centre represents
a sample location, with equal weight. It is not a residence, vehicle, workshop customer or population
estimate.

Grid design makes broad access patterns visible without using personal locations. It also samples
industrial land, water-adjacent areas and low-density fringe. A road snap can move a sample centre
away from its nominal location, so snap distance is retained.

The run reports full-grid results alongside a 250-metre source/destination snap screen. Any additional
origin-resolution checks are documented as sensitivities, not independent observations.

ROUTING METHOD



Route all listed candidates, then
examine the model assumptions
The routing workstream queried OSRM tables across the finite inventoried destination pools. It
produced 76,221 origin-destination rows, with raw responses, request logs and failed-route records
retained.

The nearest destination is selected by returned duration among OSRM-profile routes. Because the
profile can optimise a weight distinct from duration, this is not a guarantee of the globally fastest
time-minimising path. It is a consistent public model comparison.

Distances and durations come from the routing response. No arbitrary road speed converts
straight-line kilometres into minutes. The public service supplies neither observed congestion nor
workshop waiting time. A shorter journey may still lead to an unsuitable or unavailable provider.

 Check                                                                     Implementation

 Destination search                                                        All retained destinations in the stated window

 Boundary treatment                                                        Expanded destination window; core comparison retained

 Snapping                                                                  Origin and destination snap distances recorded

 Failures                                                                  Explicit files and completeness flags

 Reproduction                                                              Cached responses; reacquisition depends on service

INTERPRETATION LADDER



A clean map cannot repair uncertain
service capability
The study separates four evidentiary steps: a place is listed; a service is advertised; a journey is
modelled; a completed comparable job is verified. This run reaches the first three steps to different
degrees, but not the fourth.

The mapped candidate pool is deliberately useful for discovery and deliberately insufficient for a
definitive substitution claim. A strict requirement for independently documented Toyota/model/job
capability leaves no identified comparator set in this collection. This means the comparison is
unknown, not that no capable garages exist.

The maps therefore show where to verify alternatives and test a scoped offer. They cannot rank
workshop quality, infer owner preferences or measure causation.

COMMERCIAL INTERPRETATION



The strategy should follow the
observed constraint
The documented oil-network comparison is close to geographic parity in the Medan sample, with a
median paired gap of about 0.3 minute. It does not support treating all four markets as one large
access-deficit problem. The geographic study supports different tests in different catchments. Where
a collected Auto2000 workshop is accessible, a scoped and transparent offer may be worth testing
after capability and capacity checks. Where access is materially worse, a price-only intervention may
leave the travel burden unresolved.

Existing OtoXpert routes and economical parts should be considered before inventing a new tier. But
an operator outside Auto2000 can retain work within Toyota while producing a different Auto2000
financial result. Operator-specific contribution must be checked.

No geographic result proves that customers perceive a premium, distrust the dealer, value pickup or
would change provider. Those remain rival explanations for a subsequent pilot. The current run
deliberately excludes new customer-account/review research.

LIMITS AND UNRESOLVED QUESTIONS



What the study does not establish
The four rectangular windows and equal-weight origins are analyst choices. Provider records are
incomplete and may be wrong. Some official links conflict with addresses. Source confidence is not
service competence. The boundary margin is finite and destination entrances were not field-verified.

The routing profile does not observe traffic, queues, opening-hour alignment or appointment
availability. Nearest-by-returned-duration is a model comparison. Published service information is not
an actual accepted quote or completed job.

No eligible Toyota-owner stock, competitor market share, customer preference, switching probability,
utilisation, workshop capacity, incremental work order or realised profit is measured. The findings
should be used to prioritise verification and experiments.

The work was performed and reviewed by AI agents using public sources. Independent agent
criticism is recorded, but it is neither human peer review nor field validation. No theoretical or causal
finding is inferred merely from map density.

Interpretation. Uncertainty is attached to the claim it affects, rather than hidden in one generic
disclaimer.
