# Sources, rights and redistribution boundaries

## Existing archive
Repository: https://github.com/Fafauzif/auto-data, commit34498fe. Relevant copied files are listed and hashed in `sources/archive_input_manifest.json`. Unrelated telemetry and text-bearing review corpora are not included.

SPKLU: Wiputra,R. and Santiadi,S. (2026), A Geospatial Dataset of Public Electric Vehicle Charging Infrastructure in Indonesia, Mendeley DataV2, snapshot16June2026, https://doi.org/10.17632/z2bvd5r574.2. CC BY-NC3.0; retain attribution and noncommercial qualification. Consolidated station IDs, box records, connectors and unit counts are different units; no current operational validation is implied.

BPS/Polri: attributed public table payload-derived values for registered vehicles. Definitions and URLs accompany data; do not treat as active Toyota/xEV stock. No unrestricted license to all source wording is asserted.

## New data
Overture Maps Foundation Places release2026-10-21.0. Retain each source record's license and upstream dataset attribution fields. Most selected records share Overture/meta provenance; multiple columns do not imply independent corroboration. See provider source metadata and raw extracts. https://docs.overturemaps.org/attribution/

OpenStreetMap contributors: underlying OSRM road data, https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright. Public routing outputs are modelled routes. Do not imply live traffic, guaranteed fastest-time optimisation or business service availability. OSRM documentation: https://project-osrm.org/docs/v5.24.0/api/

Natural Earth: public-domain country outlines, https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/ ; downloaded https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/master/geojson/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson. Small-scale contextual coastlines are not local boundaries.

Auto2000, OtoXpert, Toyota Astra Motor and other workshop issuers: concise factual extracts and source locators only. No blanket right to redistribute entire current websites, source imagery or customer reviews is claimed. Source registries preserve dates and claim scope. Published directions are not field-confirmed entrances.

No simulated records are presented as source observations. Derived grids, matrices, flags and figures are original calculations on the stated inputs. Code written for this study is included for inspection and reuse subject to dependencies and source rights.
