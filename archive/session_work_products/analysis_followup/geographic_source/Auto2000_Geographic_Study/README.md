# Auto2000: geography of paid-workshop recovery

Research date: 14 November 2026 (Asia/Jakarta). Four bounded urban deep dives and fifteen contextual market windows. Public sources and free tools only. No new customer-review/account corpus, business contacts or private data.

Start with `report/Auto2000_Geographic_Workshop_Study.pdf`, then `maps/Auto2000_Geographic_Explorer.html` (standalone offline analytical explorer). Main calculations: `workstreams/routing/output/`; sensitivity calculations: adjacent routing outputs. Provider and branch evidence: corresponding `workstreams` directories. Figures: `figures/`.

## Interpretation

These are sampled grid origins and finite inventoried destinations, not customers, an eligible owner base, observed trips, an exhaustive workshop census, completed jobs or incremental profit. Official facility tags do not verify every vehicle/job. Overture category/name filters identify candidate records; they do not prove independence, Toyota fitment or competence. OSRM durations are public-profile model outputs, not live traffic. See the report and independent review for limitations.

The paid-work cohort is defined by customer payment outside applicable free/prepaid entitlements; warranty status is separate. The report may revise the initial workshop strategy rather than endorse it.

## Package scope

Includes all relevant redistributable inputs used for the calculations and figures, raw selected automotive source extracts, official-directory fact extracts, route responses, source locators, provenance, code, data dictionaries and QA. This is not a mirror of entire third-party websites. Full copyrighted pages, unrelated all-category business data, contact emails/phone fields, installed dependencies, private account data and unrelated research archives are excluded. Source extracts that remove contact fields are explicitly transformations, not exact original source bytes.

## Reproduce

Use Python3.12 or another compatible version and install `requirements.txt` into an isolated environment. Start with `REPRODUCE.md`; offline recalculation uses included data and route caches. Reacquisition requires network access and may produce a different snapshot or fail. No paid account is needed. Review all scripts before reacquisition; network stages are separate from offline processing.
