# Market context and service evidence

Study date: 14 November 2026. Research-only public-source work; no provider contact, quote submission, review collection or field verification.

## Findings that change the workshop proposition

### 1. Define the cohort by payer and entitlement, not warranty status alone

Toyota's August 2026 T-CARE XTRA release says services 8–11 are customer-paid and can earn conditional additional warranty. A customer can therefore pay for scheduled work while retaining or renewing protection. The analytical cohort should be owners with a relevant customer-paid job outside a free/prepaid service entitlement who currently use another provider. Record warranty as a separate variable. Public vehicle age or programme text cannot determine the addressable number of such owners. Source CX09.

### 2. An economical Toyota channel already exists, with different operators

OtoXpert is a manufacturer-documented alternative for light service, including oil and brake work. T-OPT's current catalogue includes oil filters and brake pads. This establishes an existing route worth comparing before proposing a new budget tier; it does not establish local stock, a matched installed price or equivalent fitment. Sources CX07–CX08.

The oil-package page advertises engine oil, gasket and filter from IDR299,000 with an approximate 30-minute duration, subject to location and vehicle conditions. This is a starting-price advertisement, not a quote for a selected Avanza or another model. It cannot support a numerical dealer-versus-independent price-gap conclusion. Source CX06.

This run extracted 37 OtoXpert directory entries across four returned pages, including 15 explicitly labelled Auto2000-operated. Other listed operators include Deltamas, Perintis, Liek Motor, Wijaya, Nasmoco and Agung Automall. Channel routing must preserve the operator: a job retained in the Toyota dealer ecosystem is not automatically an Auto2000 job. Official embedded map URLs supplied coordinate candidates, not field-verified workshop entrances. Sources CX02–CX05.

Published network totals are not reconciled. The March 2026 Toyota release states 40 OtoXpert outlets, the current T-OPT page states 38, and the four directory pages returned 37 entries. These have different dates/content states; none was silently promoted to an exact current national physical-network count. Sources CX02–CX08.

### 3. xEV programme presence is stronger than branch-by-job capability evidence

The current Toyota Hybrid Health Check page lists HEV and BEV models, a programme battery check tied to scheduled service, and a certificate. Its introductory age wording and detailed terms are not identical; the accompanying October FAQ also retains hybrid-focused wording. Eligibility should be checked by the programme and vehicle record rather than inferred from one headline. Sources CX10–CX11.

Auto2000 publishes corporate statements on hybrid technician readiness and support for Toyota electrified models sold in Indonesia. This is useful counterevidence to an assertion that no capability exists. It does not identify the workshop that can perform a particular high-voltage diagnostic or repair on a particular date. No branch-by-job accreditation or live capacity register was obtained in this workstream. Source CX12.

## Fifteen-market screening frame

`screening.csv` contains Jakarta core, Surabaya, Bandung, Medan, Palembang, Balikpapan, Denpasar, Malang, Bekasi, Bogor, Tangerang, Padang, Bandar Lampung, Pontianak and Banjarmasin. Each is represented by an explicit administrative-label selection, not an asserted official metropolitan boundary.

All have geographic presence documented in the Auto2000 directory. That directory separates showroom-only records from entries carrying workshop/parts tags, so city presence cannot be converted into a service-destination inventory without branch-level checking. Source CX01.

The existing 16 June SPKLU snapshot supplies charging-record counts within these selections. Counts describe this archive's mapped context, not service demand, Toyota ownership, BEV stock or local workshop attractiveness. Selections differ substantially in area and include some counties, so raw counts are not a comparable market ranking. Source CX13.

The four recommended deep dives are Surabaya, Medan, Bandung and Palembang. The branch-research workstream obtained official city directories for these markets. The purposive contrast covers an East Java core, Bandung core/fringe, north Sumatra urban region, and Palembang river-crossing setting. Each has an official economical-channel listing; several have multiple operators. This is a feasible contrast design, not a claim that these are the four most profitable markets. Jakarta remains in the screening frame but is excluded from the primary comparison for practical comparative scope, not because its opportunity is lower. Independent-provider completeness and routing feasibility remain separate screens to be supplied by the other workstreams.

Palembang provides the river-crossing case, Balikpapan remains a source-coordinate reserve, and the capital-region fringes offer a way to test whether core-city conclusions persist beyond administrative boundaries. Bandar Lampung and Pontianak have no OtoXpert record in the pages returned here; this is not verified absence.

## Interpreting the maps

A branch map should show at least three different concepts: public branch identity, a documented general workshop service tag, and job-specific qualification. Collapsing them makes the closest destination appear more capable than the evidence establishes.

An economical-channel layer should distinguish Auto2000-operated OtoXpert from other operators. It can be included in a scenario for Auto2000-group destinations only when operator identity supports it. Do not infer accounting treatment, contribution-sharing or CRM integration from the name alone.

A generic independent-garage map is a candidate-alternative map. Its density can describe mapped competitive context, but cannot show that every workshop accepts the selected Toyota or performs the same brake job with equivalent parts. Job-verified comparisons require tighter evidence. If those data are unavailable, the exploratory and qualified analyses must remain explicitly different.

Public accessibility cannot identify owners' current provider, due jobs, price acceptance, booked capacity or realised contribution. Therefore, a spatial disadvantage is an operational hypothesis, not evidence of lost paid work. Conversely, short modelled travel is not evidence that a dealer's price or confirmation process is satisfactory.

## Public-data evidence gaps retained

- No matched provider quote for one specified vehicle/model year/job across these markets.
- No verified outlet stock, installed T-OPT fitment or available appointment.
- No branch-by-job xEV accreditation roster.
- No owner-level warranty, entitlement, service-history or current-provider data.
- No inferred paid-job volume, switching rate or margin forecast.
- No current OtoXpert network total reconciled across the differing publications.

The appropriate strategy test is an operator-aware, job-specific service offer with verified destination and confirmation. Whether it should target petrol paid jobs, economical-channel routing or high-stakes xEV support depends on completed job and contribution evidence beyond these public maps.
