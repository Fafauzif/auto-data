## The business question

Identify where Auto2000 can capture incremental, economically meaningful ownership transactions across three prongs:

1. Acquire xEV buyers who are new to Auto2000. This is not necessarily a first-ever vehicle or first-ever EV purchase.
2. Recover customer-paid aftersales work, including older petrol Toyota owners using independent workshops, while supporting HEV/PHEV/BEV ownership needs.
3. Retain existing Auto2000 xEV owners through an executable replacement or trade-in transaction.

The case places xEV sales and aftersales at its center. The broader petrol paid-work opportunity must support a coherent case response rather than displace the xEV question. Recommendations may change priorities when evidence warrants it.

The case investment ceiling is IDR 150 billion for 2026–2028. It is a ceiling, not a required spending target. Case aftersales margins aggregate powertrains and cannot be relabeled xEV margins. Customers, origin-grid points, reviews, tweets, charger records and telemetry values are different units.

## How to use previous strategy

Treat the earlier three-prong architecture as a question structure. Evaluate each proposed feature against existing Auto2000/Toyota capabilities. Booking, test-drive requests, trade-in/appraisal journeys and battery-health evidence have existing implementations. Distinct value requires transaction completion, accepted evidence and fulfillment, rather than inventing a capability that already exists.

Use all-channel incremental completed paid jobs and recognized gross profit rather than app-only migration or monthly active users as the principal economic outcomes. Keep requests, confirmations, attendance, authorized work, completed work, payment and repeat transactions distinct. Record payer and free/prepaid entitlement separately from warranty status.

## Analytical standard

Write firm recommendations within the population and comparison actually observed. Each material insight should connect evidence, mechanism, competing explanation and a changed choice. Keep source facts, model outputs, customer accounts, interpretation, scenarios and recommendations distinct. Do not invent causal sales uplift, observed margin, customer demand, workshop capacity or current prices.

Original text is preserved. Translations and coding are derivatives. Keyword coding is a reproducible screening method, not a validated sentiment classifier or proof of customer identity. Review publication date is not necessarily visit/event date. A model origin is not a household, Toyota owner or demand-weighted customer.
