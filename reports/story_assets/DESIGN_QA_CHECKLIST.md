# Visual memo design and QA checklist

## Reference boundary

The four-page uploaded audit PDF is a visual reference only. Its useful traits are short finding-led sections, large evidence panels, brief interpretation directly beneath charts, local source captions and generous whitespace. Its competition, products, figures, quotations, people and factual argument must not enter the Auto2000 report. Do not reuse its screenshots, logos or decorative product artwork.

The memo should borrow the reading rhythm, not reproduce the reference. Auto2000 conclusions must come from this project's evidence registers. The complete research archive remains the place for exhaustive tables, code and long source lists.

## Page contract

- Give each page one finding, stated in a plain-English headline of about 6-12 words. A reader should understand the conclusion before reading the body.
- Target 100-200 total explanatory words per main page. Use 60-100 words of body prose when a dominant chart or evidence panel supplies the rest. Do not shrink text to fit the old long report.
- Use two or three short paragraphs, usually one to three sentences each. Explain the result, its implication and its boundary in that order.
- Aim for a dominant visual occupying roughly half the usable page on at least half the memo's pages. The visual should carry evidence, rather than decorate a text-heavy page.
- Keep a small, consistent page number and section label. A section divider must help navigation, not consume a page that could carry a finding.
- Retain the user's solution choice. The final menu compares qualified alternatives without a winning strategy, implementation plan or rollout.

## Typography and spacing

- Use a real body size of at least 12 pt, with comfortable leading. Use at least 10 pt for source and uncertainty captions, and preferably 11-12 pt for chart labels.
- Keep headlines near 24-30 pt and subheads near 15-17 pt. Avoid long all-capital headings and several competing font sizes.
- Use deliberate whitespace between title, interpretation, visual and source note. Do not stretch a short paragraph across the entire page solely to fill space.
- Keep headings with their following paragraph. Keep at least two lines of a paragraph on both sides of a page break. Remove one-word widows such as the earlier standalone "market."
- Add spaces around numeric prose: "59 remaining months", "9 million listings", "returned 403". Preserve exact evidence IDs, model identifiers and URLs.
- Use embedded fonts. Test every special character. Prefer words such as "to" for rank transitions if a glyph is unsupported. Never deliver missing-character boxes.

## Evidence and chart contract

- Put the source ID, observation date, population or question base, unit and key limitation beside the claim or chart. A distant bibliography cannot carry a missing denominator.
- Distinguish observations, published claims and hypotheses in words. For journeys, "observed page state" is different from a completed customer transaction.
- Give every chart a finding-led title and explicit units. Use a zero baseline for ordinary bars. Explain a logarithmic axis directly on the chart when it is analytically necessary.
- Label important values directly. Round consistently for display while preserving exact numbers in the underlying data. Never cut off labels, bars, error ranges or footnotes.
- Use two panels when quantities have different units or scales. Do not make upfront cash and a monthly instalment appear to share one financial meaning.
- Prefer a compact evidence panel to a chart when the finding is a label mismatch, a stated programme rule or an observed boundary. Do not invent a metric to obtain a plot.
- Use a narrow, consistent palette. Color can distinguish scenarios, but text labels must also explain them. Avoid red/green alone.
- Do not use screenshots or invented app interfaces as decoration. A redrawn evidence table or state diagram must be labelled as a research redraw, not presented as the product UI.
- Never imply that source-row counts are customers, that source URLs are independent studies, or that descriptive differences establish causal effects.

## Journey-specific guardrails

- Say that the particular desktop task worked, rather than claiming universal usability. Preserve city, model, variant and the safe endpoint.
- Label a VIN/plate boundary as untested downstream access. Do not call a required identifier unnecessary without further evidence.
- For the finance chart, keep the same vehicle, city, price, base DP, first-instalment timing and tenure. Only the insurance-payment timing changes. These are conditional illustrations, not binding quotes or hidden-fee findings.
- For the BEV mismatch, preserve the displayed value 7311 and translated label. Do not replace it with an assumed correct engineering value or diagnose a software cause.
- For test drives, separate preview, submission, staff confirmation and attendance. Only the preview and public FAQ were inspected. The two observed branch/model handoffs provide a counterexample to a blanket lost-context claim.
- Report zero mobile-emulated and zero native-app journeys. The separate local launch failed before navigation; native DevTools was blocked by organization policy; no available native emulator was identified. These are environment limits, not failures measured on Auto2000's mobile website or app.

## Visual acceptance pass

1. Inspect the final PDF page by page at normal reading size, then open every dense chart, table and source note at full resolution.
2. Check headline hierarchy, reading order, body size, line spacing, contrast, page numbering and whitespace.
3. Check every chart's units, selected base, scenario labels and displayed values against its frozen data file. Confirm that annotations do not imply a customer outcome that was not observed.
4. Check all page edges, table cells, wrapped URLs and diagram labels for clipping or overlap. Check the rendered glyphs, not only extracted text or bounding boxes.
5. Confirm that each finding has a nearby source and uncertainty statement. Keep full reproducibility detail in the ZIP instead of re-expanding the memo.
6. Review the solution-menu family pages for readable, distinct cards. Preserve all 27 concepts without implying that they are 27 additive profit pools or independent causal findings.
7. Record the reviewed file hash, page coverage, AI-review status and any remaining limitations. Visual review is not a substitute for human review or fresh source verification.
