# Auto2000 Digiroom interview analysis

**Decision as of 16 November 2026.** Test a Digiroom service record that an Auto2000 adviser can open from a normal WhatsApp conversation and that a driver can share with the person authorized to approve paid work. Start with a tightly scoped Yaris Cross Hybrid service cohort at Auto2000 Soekarno-Hatta, Bandung, conditional on the branch confirming it can support the workflow and measure the outcome. Use confirmed household-fit acquisition and inspected net trade-in offers as follow-on paths in the same ownership record.

The original IBSC case requires digital innovation, meaningful regular Digiroom use, and xEV sales and aftersales gross profit within the IDR 150 billion 2026–2028 ceiling. These interviews change what the shared record should do and where to test it. Three qualitative interviews supply mechanisms and counterexamples. They supply no estimate of market prevalence, paid-job conversion, app adoption, or incremental profit.

## Evidence and method

The evidence consists of two filled interviewer guides, six user-supplied Atto answer notes, and one unfilled Digiroom guide that documents the interview protocol and a candidate pilot. The DOCX contributes method and proposed stimuli. The supplied Atto notes have uncertain verbatim status, so the analysis paraphrases them. Interview dates, recording files, and independent transaction records were unavailable in the materials analyzed. The source register preserves the provided permission scope, provenance, and original files for private use.

The analysis reconstructs actions and outcomes, labels the respondent's source of knowledge, and compares episodes where the existing method worked or where behavior changed. A positive service record in a filled guide and the guide author's interpretation both trace to the same session. Case evidence, social accounts, modeled geography, official capability observations, and financial scenarios retain their respective units. ## Acquisition depends on the household's operating conditions and who decides

**A charging problem becomes actionable when it changes a trip.** The Atto case has two such changes, a rideshare substitution in a time-constrained episode and a charger search near Rungkut. The Wuling case provides the opposing condition, an apartment charger that absorbs waiting time into the existing routine. The contrast concerns access and time flexibility. The respondents' home charging arrangements are known only in the Wuling case, and the time, station used and actual charging completion in Rungkut are unknown. A charging-station count therefore cannot distinguish these cases by itself.

Compare this with X SX05, which raises resilience in a sole-car household, and YouTube SY02, where a commenter reportedly used public charging after choosing against installing a wall charger. The Atto case adds behavior under urgency; it gives no count of available plugs or verified outage. The corrected Sidoarjo geography G2 and G3 models workshop access and branch assignment. It makes the southern corridor worth distinguishing in location planning, while its modeled origins cannot validate public-charging congestion, routes driven, or demand. The general preference for PLN does not identify the Rungkut charging provider.

**Product fit and deal execution need different diagnoses.** The Atto driver recalls comparing Wuling, perceiving an attractive price and comparable quality, and wanting to try an EV. Parents transacted, and any offers they rejected remain unknown. This is competitor consideration and personal preference, with no documented Toyota/Auto2000 lead lost. The Yaris case instead ties household selection to reported fuel efficiency versus its Raize and an established sales relationship. Maps M15 and M16 show separate examples of remote purchase logistics, finance and handover. X SX02 reports an unavailable test-drive unit at an unspecified outlet and vehicle. The two interviews neither erase this specific execution hurdle nor show that families always reach it. Archive finance exercises UX03–UX04 and XT02 illustrate meaningful changes in upfront and monthly cash, but the interviews do not verify a binding cash or lender constraint.

**Acquisition decision.** Continue the confirmed, model-matched demonstration and offer path. Give each actor something they can act on. The daily driver should be able to check common trips, access and fallback costs. The buyer should be able to see the real quote, cash due, finance status and next action. Use charging planning when the BEV household's route or usable access calls for it. The evidence supports an acquisition test measured through attendance, qualified progression and delivery, with first Toyota, first xEV, and first Auto2000 separately recorded. No new-to-Auto2000 delivery is shown by these three interviews.

## Service gains depend on a specific handoff and eligible work

**WhatsApp already closes routine work for the two drivers who described service.** Wuling Q02, Q05, Q06 and Q09 show a driver who arranges work, accepts it and pays before family reimbursement. Yaris Q02, Q05, Q06 and Q09 show a driver who completes a covered visit using branch WhatsApp while the father retains authority over extra cost. The second driver would revisit a service record for work details or approval status in the concept test; they also reported no current problem worth fixing. The Wuling driver saw no added value in an app for the completed task. These are conditions under which compulsory app booking risks moving a completed transaction between channels.

**The stronger problem is incomplete handoff when the job needs it.** Maps M01 and M03 describe a paid-intent arrival and a walk-in registration failure. M08 and M09 describe repeat or forgotten work at Auto2000 Soekarno-Hatta. M06 and M12–M14 give relevant contrasts on branch relationships, work explanation and entitlement changes. The productive and failed workflows identify different conditions for testing the handoff. The prior Deloitte service-choice tables and the public and legacy service reports reinforce the importance of advisor relationships, work quality and continuity. They cannot establish the frequency of these failures in the proposed HEV cohort.

**Aftersales decision.** Keep the normal WhatsApp entry point and test a case the customer can return to in Digiroom. It should show who received the job, the correct entitlement and estimate basis, the accepted scope and expected finish, a versioned request for extra-work approval, and completion or escalation. An authorised approver receives a narrowly scoped case view only with appropriate authorization and revocable access. This gives Digiroom an ownership task beyond duplicating booking and lets a completed covered job remain covered. Existing booking and CRM capabilities from the journey audits remain the baseline for technical design. The Atto report of no immobilizing fault leaves routine service and support-channel outcomes unknown.

## Trade-in interest points to different stages of the decision

The Yaris respondent actually searched marketplace and dealer prices, reaching a reference range. They expressed interest in a better-supported appraisal process, with no documented inspection, offer or settlement. The Atto respondent had taken no resale action, viewed perceived running savings as an offset to possible depreciation, and stated that an upfront price floor would increase purchase confidence. The Atto case justifies testing the effect of certainty on an actual buyer. It does not identify how the parents would act, what conditions or price they would accept, or whether it would affect delivery. The Wuling driver reported a family vehicle change managed by the father and could not describe its terms.

X SX06 and SX07 offer a different response to resale worries, personal saving within one thread. YouTube SY03 and SY06 raise support continuity as a rival explanation for resale decisions. Kertajaya M19 and M20 mention TRIVA appraisal and comparison functions, while M21–M24 illustrate dependencies among accepted evidence, deposits, finance and replacement readiness. Existing health-check and trade-in functionality in R1 and the prior capability and battery work makes another estimate or certificate a weak novelty claim. The single-vehicle Audi telemetry source cannot validate Toyota health evidence or a resale premium. The case decision is what an actual appraisal operator will accept and put into a usable offer.

**Trade-in decision.** Test a written, inspected net-upgrade offer with expiry, evidence requirements, fees, outstanding settlement, firm replacement availability and a named owner for each handoff. When that workflow can be fulfilled, compare its effect to a bounded, funded top-up before considering a full floor. In the guide's constructed example, a Rp160m inspected value and Rp10m cap credit Rp170m, while a qualifying Rp200m floor requires Rp40m of additional value. An assumed insurer layer is a separate recovery after a promise is made. It cannot establish cover, shrink the customer's promise, or justify a launch. The full floor needs buyer behavior at known terms, an actual promisor, eligible conditions, settlement rules, funded exposure under correlated resale declines, and an approved post-horizon obligation. Existing economics scenarios retain their original denominators and assumptions.

### Pilot design and launch gates

1. **Confirm branch and eligible opportunities.** Appoint a branch service adviser and supervisor, plus Digiroom and finance/measurement owners. Confirm Yaris Cross Hybrid work categories the branch accepts, appointment and parts readiness, case creation rights, payer/entitlement capture, data access, and sufficient volume. Start with due vehicle-specific routine service on eligible Yaris Cross Hybrid vehicles. Track wholly covered cases separately. Define paid or mixed eligibility before assignment from the booked job, entitlement and pre-existing scope. Capture newly discovered paid additions in all assigned cases as a secondary outcome. Use the same eligibility rules and window for both arms. Use a recent branch baseline to define realistic thresholds and an escalation window; do not use these interviews for incidence assumptions.
2. **Test the added digital record.** In both arms customers can use normal WhatsApp or assisted service. Where feasible, assign eligible households or a pre-specified branch-week rollout to usual practice or usual practice plus the Digiroom record and approval handoff. Compare at assignment level with explicit contamination controls, scheduled capacity and denominator freezes. Preserve an assisted approval option and a single work-order outcome. If credible assignment or comparable cohort data are unavailable, label results descriptive.
3. **Measure completed work first, and meaningful Digiroom return alongside it.** Primary measure for the commercial comparison is completed customer-paid work orders per eligible paid or mixed opportunity, with recognized Auto2000 contribution and a defined comparison period. Track acknowledged arrivals, authorization completion, time to decision, repeat visits, rework, disputes, customer effort, and adviser time. Report covered completion and reimbursement separately. In Digiroom, measure voluntary return for a distinct status, approval, completion record or subsequent eligible ownership event. Count each work order once across channels and log variable cost, displaced work and actual contribution. Review sample size and operating readiness before launch; pause expansion if authorization reliability, service quality, or contribution fails the pre-agreed gates.
4. **Extend through the same record after proof.** Add model-confirmed demonstration and executable appraisal as separate tests with delivered xEV and completed replacement endpoints. Preserve time-to-event differences and budget gates. A completed visit, app reopen, appraisal request, or customer preference each serves a distinct purpose in evaluation.

The interviews narrow the use of charging support, strengthen the need to preserve functioning WhatsApp workflows and split the roles of driver, buyer, payer and approver, and sharpen the sequence from market estimate to executable offer.
