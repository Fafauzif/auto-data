# Geographic decisions after integrating the latest evidence

Research cut-off: 14 November 2026. This report independently recomputes the latest corrected routing release and interprets it alongside newly supplied branch narratives. It supersedes the original geographic conclusions wherever the two disagree. Original reports, scripts and evidence remain preserved rather than silently rewritten.

## The decision is where to protect completion and where to reduce travel

Auto2000 should use two allocation rules. Protect reception, accepted slots, parts readiness and completion at branches whose removal materially weakens general-workshop access. Target job-specific travel reduction where a gap remains after corrected coordinates and Auto2000-operated channels are included. These are different decisions: a branch can have little residual oil-access disadvantage yet be important to the general-workshop network. Conversely, a large residual oil-access gap does not automatically establish that the branch needs more bays or that a new facility is warranted.

The new portfolio result is precise. Across 284 retained paired calculation origins in the four windows, corrected Auto2000 general-workshop routing has 39 origins more than five model minutes behind the collected oil-network comparator. Permitting eligible jobs to use Auto2000-operated OtoXpert reduces this to 20. Bandung contains 14 of those 20 remaining model origins, with eight assigned to Soekarno-Hatta. Medan and Palembang each have zero remaining origins above that threshold. This is an allocation description of the finite model, not a distribution of customers, demand or recoverable gross profit.

The five-minute line is an analytical grouping. It is not a measured consumer tolerance or a proposed universal service target. The network substitution is a counterfactual change in permitted endpoints. Its value is that existing, correctly operated locations can serve the modeled geography; it is not evidence of realized referrals or incremental jobs.

## 1. Concentrate residual access work in Bandung, rather than distributing pickup across four cities

Bandung retains 14 of the 20 residual tail origins after corrected Suci and Auto2000-operated OtoXpert are included. Soekarno-Hatta accounts for eight, Suci three, and Cibiru, Pasteur and Setiabudi one each. Surabaya's remaining six fall in HR Muhammad, Waru and Manyar. No residual cell above the analytical threshold remains in Medan or Palembang. This turns a four-city access proposal into a selective job-routing and travel-burden programme.

For Soekarno-Hatta, the appropriate first offer is an eligible oil/filter job through existing THS where the location can be served, or one accepted onsite visit with parts ready and a completion window. The additional shifted-grid and building-anchor tests retain a positive travel disadvantage: the selected six-point stress sets have a median optimized outbound gap of approximately 3.557 minutes and a roundtrip gap of 7.098 minutes. These are targeted robustness tests rather than population-weighted estimates.

The newly supplied branch evidence supports the mechanism, independently of the routing arithmetic. Maps case M09 reports a complaint omitted from the first service and a next-day return, followed by another episode in which work started only after escalation. M08 praises friendliness, punctuality and the waiting room while describing five unresolved visits. M08 is historical diagnostic evidence published in July 2024; M09 was published in October 2025 and occurs in both the latest and diagnostic runs. Neither is an observed route or proof that the modeled origins were the reviewers' homes. Together, they make visit completion a better intervention target than a hospitality-only score or an extra booking button.

Decision: use Soekarno-Hatta as the strongest named residual-access and resolution pilot in this evidence set. Require the job complaint to survive intake, authorization and completion checks. Make pickup a priced residual option subject to contribution, rather than an automatic city-wide subsidy.

## 2. Preserve Pancing and Tanjung Api-Api even when oil-access gaps disappear

Pancing has zero residual oil-tail origins after owned-channel routing, but eight retained origins lose modeled general-workshop access within 15 minutes if Pancing is removed. Tanjung Api-Api likewise has zero residual tails after OtoXpert is permitted, but seven origins lose that general-workshop threshold without TAA. These are leave-one-out network comparisons, not measured outage probabilities or capacity values. They identify why oil routing and general-workshop continuity should be managed separately.

The contrast with Soekarno-Hatta is useful: it retains eight residual tail origins but has zero origins uniquely dependent on it for the 15-minute general-workshop threshold. Its case therefore comes from residual task-specific burden and service resolution, rather than a large count of uniquely protected cells. That does not imply closure, spare capacity or irrelevance for other jobs.

Pancing M03, published March 2026 and present in both latest and diagnostic cohorts, describes a non-booking arrival whose vehicle was not entered into the queue and who waited from about 09.00 to 13.30. This case changes the design: reception acknowledgment must cover walk-ins as well as booked arrivals. Historical positive Pancing M14 describes estimates and approval before replacement despite perceived higher prices. The contrast makes reception, estimate acceptance and work progression better supported priorities than a blanket discount.

TAA M11 describes uncertainty over progress and collection; M31 reports a braking concern after a service that included a brake check. Those are self-reports and do not establish mechanical causation. They nevertheless show why rerouting simple oil work should not substitute for scope ownership and a clear completion handover on work that remains with the general workshop.

Decision: distinguish a general-workshop service-preservation mandate from a residual-access mandate. Pancing and TAA should have accepted ownership, queue entry, readiness and collection controls; eligible oil jobs can use the owned economical channel without pretending that the entire general-workshop workload has moved.

## 3. Correcting facility data changes capital priorities before any vehicle moves

Adding corrected Suci changes the nearest branch for 20 of 78 retained Bandung origins. It reduces the median oil-network gap from approximately 2.636 to 1.736 minutes and reduces the tail count from 21 to 17. Cibiru's assigned origins fall from 22 to 14 and its tail origins from four to one. A proposed access project based on the earlier Cibiru picture would therefore include a facility-recording problem in its investment case.

The correction is traceable. The original official directions pointed at the Setiabudi location despite Suci's PHH Mustofa address. The alternate public map coordinate agrees with the address-concordant Overture point within about nine metres. The selected location is a published facility-point candidate, not an entrance claimed to have been surveyed. Its road snap of approximately 30.64 metres passes the study's 250-metre screen.

Suci also has three origins uniquely protected at the modeled 15-minute general-workshop threshold. It belongs in both the facility-data correction and service-preservation discussion. This is why a metadata correction should not be treated as a purely technical appendix: it changes which customer-facing destination is offered and which geographic problem remains worth funding.

Decision: put a versioned facility registry ahead of access spending. A facility record needs a branch identity, address, coordinate provenance, job/facility hours, operator and supported referral path. A corrected record must invalidate stale routing outputs explicitly.

## 4. Keep Sidoarjo in southern routing rather than treating the old study boundary as the market

Sidoarjo has no nearest-origin assignment on the original Surabaya grid. That grid ends north of the corrected branch. In six targeted southern building-anchor origins, all six choose corrected Sidoarjo, with a median roundtrip oil-network gap of approximately 0.021 minutes. This is a direct boundary-sensitivity result, not evidence of a new six-customer market or a representative sample of southern demand.

Decision: retain Sidoarjo as a southern routing choice and correct its coordinate. Do not rank it below branches inside the original rectangle based on zero assigned cells. The same rule applies to other zero-assignment endpoints: zero winners require a boundary and objective review before any commercial deprioritization.

## 5. Use job, operator and time as routing gates

The oil comparator contains the collected OtoXpert and Shop&Drive network locations, including operators outside Auto2000. The owned scenario permits Auto2000 general workshops and OtoXpert Rungkut, Ring Road Medan, Peta, Bojongsoang and Palembang, according to the preserved official operator labels. Toyota affiliation or Astra group membership does not make the transaction Auto2000 gross profit. A transfer from a general Auto2000 workshop to its own OtoXpert is one reassigned job, not two incremental jobs.

Time can change the feasible endpoint even when distance barely differs. Cilandak publishes Sunday showroom hours but a closed workshop; the preserved OtoXpert directory publishes Sunday operating hours for Auto2000-operated Fatmawati. At the selected bridge origin, Fatmawati's roundtrip is only 0.93 minutes longer than Cilandak's. The oil job should be routed using workshop hours and acceptance, not a generic dealer-open flag. Unknown hours must remain unknown rather than being treated as closed.

The reference job is an explicitly scoped Avanza 2019 1.3 petrol oil/filter job paid outside free or prepaid entitlements. It is a comparison design, not the empirically dominant model in these catchments. Front-pad replacement needs its own fitment, kit, labour, tax and authorization pathway. General THS inspection language does not establish that front-pad replacement or high-voltage diagnosis can be done at home.

Current November promotional evidence strengthens the case that Shop&Drive is a relevant network comparator, but does not establish a current like-for-like installed price. The 13–16 November four-litre offer and 4–26 November filter promotion have different terms; the Rp638k transmission bundle is a different job. The brake-pad payday windows are not active on 14 November. The T-OPT Avanza-family front-pad part price is from July 2024 and excludes a current installed quote. A price-premium percentage must not be manufactured from these incompatible offers.

Decision: route by eligible job, operator, facility time and accepted scope before optimizing the modeled travel duration. Actual slot, stock, fitment and contribution stay transaction fields rather than assumptions inferred from a map marker.

## 6. One journey-control backbone can serve all three prongs

The geographic result is strongest for defined workshop tasks. It does not turn charger density into evidence of xEV diagnostic competence or make the petrol-service opportunity a substitute for the case's xEV objectives. The latest package preserves fourteen official xEV sources and eight named endpoint paths. They show existing HHC, model/branch-bearing test-drive requests and inspection-to-offer trade-in pathways.

Use the same accepted-handoff logic with different completion events. A first-Auto2000 xEV prospect needs a confirmed model/variant, unit, location, adviser and appointment followed by actual attendance, offer and delivery. An xEV owner needs eligibility, receiving case owner, suitable endpoint, result and payer/warranty decision. A repeat buyer needs the inspected offer, remaining debt, net equity and actual replacement quote. Charging availability is supporting context for some BEV decisions; it is not the universal receiving endpoint for all three tasks.

Decision: share context and acknowledgment infrastructure across the three prongs, while keeping their commercial outcomes and payer rules separate. Do not count a submitted request, app-channel migration, included HHC check or internal referral as incremental gross profit.

## Reproduction and audit

The independent standard-library audit reads the original 76,221 main OD rows, 5,913 supplementary rows and 162 corrected outbound rows. The corrected source also retains the separately calculated reverse directions. It derives nearest endpoints after the joint origin/destination snap screen of 250 metres, classifies OtoXpert operators from the official inventory and recomputes the corrected branch table. Across 29 endpoints and ten fields per endpoint, all 290 checks match the saved release within 1e-9 minutes where numerical comparison applies. The strict paired origin counts are 59,78,78,69. No network requests were used.

Tie handling is explicit. Stable first-minimum assignment reproduces the published partition, but `tie_ids` retains all exact tied general-workshop endpoints. At TAA/Veteran, the geographic tail attribution is seven uniquely TAA origins plus one shared origin, not eight uniquely TAA origins. Possible upper-bound counts across branches must not be summed as distinct cells.

Run `scripts/independent_od_audit.py --source-root PATH_TO_AUTO2000_LANJUTAN --output OUTPUT_DIR`, then `scripts/portfolio_analysis.py --table PATH_TO_BRANCH_DECISION_TABLE_29 --output OUTPUT_DIR`. These scripts only read the saved data and write the requested results. The final repo may relocate the original source tree; explicit CLI paths remain supported.
