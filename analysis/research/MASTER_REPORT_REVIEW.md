## Accounting qualifications to add

The proposed `incremental_GP=T×a×g` is valid for a volume-only calculation under constant economics on transactions that would occur anyway, or when all contribution changes and subsidies to those other transactions are separately included. It is not a complete general GP bridge if existing captured jobs also receive discounts or their unit economics change. Add: **'This normalized form holds other completed-job economics constant; include contribution changes on nonincremental transactions separately, or use the full with-change minus counterfactual ledger.'** Otherwise the report's own universal-subsidy criticism is not fully represented by the formula.

Also add: **'Set displaced-contribution term d to zero where displacement is already reflected in the all-channel counterfactual/incremental share; recognize it once.'** The present definition includes d but does not explicitly protect against that double count.

The150bn/3=50bn annual simple-recovery scale test is arithmetically correct. The100,000/50,000annual jobs at assumed500,000/1,000,000contribution are also correct. They are appropriately labeled illustrations, not operational targets. Keep 'contribution' distinct from the previous paragraph's GP, since the500,000assumption changes meaning after variable costs. One clarifying phrase suffices: 'at an assumed500,000 **net contribution after the included variable costs** per genuinely incremental job'.
