Glossary (1/2)
                                 Term                                              Definition

EV (Electric Vehicle)                   In this case, any vehicle with an electric drivetrain: HEV, PHEV or BEV.

HEV (Hybrid Electric Vehicle)           Petrol engine plus electric motor; the battery charges while driving and cannot be plugged in.

PHEV (Plug-in Hybrid)                   A hybrid with a larger battery that can also be charged from a plug.

BEV (Battery Electric Vehicle)          Powered only by a battery and electric motor, with no petrol engine.

ICE (Internal Combustion Engine)        A conventional vehicle powered only by a fuel-burning engine.

Wholesales                              Vehicles sold by the manufacturer or distributor to dealers.

Market share                            Sales of a brand or vehicle type as a percentage of total national sales.

GIIAS                                   Gaikindo Indonesia International Auto Show, Indonesia’s main annual car exhibition.

SPK (Surat Pesanan Kendaraan)           The vehicle order form a customer signs to confirm a purchase.

4S                                      Dealer business model covering Sales, Service, Spare parts and Body & Paint.

Toyota Sales Operation                  The Toyota dealer business of PT Astra International Tbk, run under the Auto2000 brand.

B2C / B2B                               B2C: sales to individual customers. B2B: sales to companies, including fleet buyers.

Glossary (2/2)
                             Term                                                        Definition

After-sales                                Revenue earned after the vehicle sale: service, spare parts, and body & paint.

Home Service                               A Toyota technician services the car at the customer’s chosen location.

Trade-in                                   Using a customer’s current car as part payment for a new one.

OLXmobbi                                   Used-car platform that values trade-in vehicles for Auto2000.

Kinto                                      Toyota’s car subscription service: a monthly fee to use a car instead of buying it.

                                           Auto2000’s app for car purchase, service booking, test drive, trade-in, loyalty and tracking,
Digiroom
                                           relaunched 6 December 2025.

MAU (Monthly Active Users)                 Unique users who actively use the app in a given month.

Session                                    One visit in which a user opens and uses the app.

CRM (Customer Relationship Management)     Systems used to manage and analyze customer data and interactions.

Single ID                                  CRM feature that combines all of a customer’s interactions into one profile.

Gross profit (GP) and gross margin (GM%)   GP is revenue minus the direct cost of what was sold; GM% is GP as a percentage of revenue.

Operating profit                           Gross profit minus operating expenses such as salaries, rent and marketing.
