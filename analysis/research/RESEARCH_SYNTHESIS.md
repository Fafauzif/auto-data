# Make the next ownership decision executable, then count the completed business

Integrated interpretation of the repository research, prepared 14 November 2026. This document covers the pre-existing research frozen on 11 November, the supplied mentoring notes, and its implications for the new integrated release. Newly scraped X, YouTube, Google Maps and the latest workshop geography have separate analyses in this release. They should be read together rather than added as independent confirmations of repeated source material.

## The strategic choice

Auto2000 should organize the three prongs around an executable customer decision: a suitable vehicle and confirmed demonstration for acquisition; an eligible, correctly routed and completed job for after-sales; and an inspected, accepted net-upgrade offer for replacement. Digiroom is a coordination and decision surface within these journeys. Its success is the additional completed business it enables across channels, not an obligation to turn infrequent ownership tasks into daily app use.

This is more specific than a generic demand for convenience. The alternative is commercially plausible: invest in new app utilities, more content, recurring engagement and stronger monetary promises to stimulate demand. The existing evidence favors improving decision quality and fulfillment first because many proposed utilities already exist, vehicle-connected engagement depends on OEM controls, and a residual-value promise creates obligations that an information product does not remove. This recommendation is a ranked interpretation of the evidence, not a claim that a controlled Auto2000 outcome experiment has already succeeded.

The old repository stopped at diagnosis and a menu of alternatives. The user's 14 November instruction now permits integrated recommendations, deeper analysis and a complete handoff. Historical language such as 'do not select a winner' describes the earlier commission. It must not silently override this release's mandate.

## The original case requires a digital xEV answer, with ordinary paid work as supporting economics

The original casebook directly requires differentiated digital innovation that gives EV owners and buyers reasons for regular Digiroom use, links engagement to EV sales and after-sales GP, and includes cost, feasibility, risks and an implementation roadmap (p6). Its definition of EV includes HEV, PHEV and BEV (p18). A petrol-only workshop recovery programme or a general branch-operations memo would therefore be incomplete even if its economics were attractive. The proposed digital case-state coordination must visibly serve the xEV acquisition, ownership and replacement pathways. Ordinary paid Toyota work can support near-term learning and commercial scale without replacing the case mandate.

The case supplies a stronger starting point than app inactivity alone. Digiroom has about 21,000 MAU (range20,000–22,000) and 1.6 sessions per active user monthly; its service-booking channel share is16%, while WhatsApp accounts for 38% and walk-in/phone46% (p11). These denominators differ. The CRM stakeholder explicitly says Single ID exists but sales teams cannot use it easily yet (p7). Consequently, the digital innovation can be an actionable, permissioned case brief and fulfillment state across existing channels, rather than a claim that the company needs its first unified identity.

Regular use should be earned through meaningful progress, due care and decision events. The proposed retention loop is not 'accept low MAU and do nothing.' It is a sequence of useful state changes that brings the customer back when a decision, confirmation, due job, result, offer or exception needs action, while tracking whether the sequence produces completed profitable business. The roadmap should test both meaningful active use and all-channel outcomes. It should not optimize app sessions by making tasks harder to finish.

The judging weights reinforce the priority: strategic solution30%, impact/feasibility30%, problem analysis20%, data/evidence15% and design5% (p15). Archive completeness supports evidence; it cannot substitute for a coherent strategy and a realistic commercial mechanism. The original submission guide limits the competition paper to 15 main pages excluding cover/references/appendix (p14). This research release can be longer, with a future competition submission distilled from it.

## 1. Auto2000 already owns much of the infrastructure; the differentiator is the promise it can finish

The capability register documents SAM, Reminder Service, Mobile Service Advisor, Single ID/Customer 360, Digiroom purchase/service/test-drive/trade-in routes, loyalty and assisted channels. It also records 31 Auto2000 outlets with an integrated EV ecosystem in the cited 2025 annual report. This is a substantial baseline for a dealer business. A proposal to build the first booking route, first credit calculator, first service history or first battery certificate would misdiagnose the starting point.

The public desktop journeys provide stronger evidence than a feature list alone. Hybrid and BEV finance previews worked before contact entry. Two actual branch/model links preserved the selected branch and vehicle, including a control route that changed both selections. Broad workshop filters, guest routes and a BEV model selector also existed. These counterexamples rule out universal claims of missing self-service or lost branch context. They leave a narrower and commercially useful question: when does a request acquire a responsible owner, a feasible offer and a completed result?

The first implementation priority should therefore be an explicit state and exception contract using existing channels. Each relevant journey needs to distinguish requested, received, under review, confirmed, attended, authorized, completed and closed. A response should name who can decide the next step and what changes the status. The shortest useful implementation may be process and information changes around current systems. ERP completion is not a prerequisite for every improvement, while a corporate ERP announcement does not establish that every branch already exposes the needed fields.

## 2. The investment horizon makes the installed vehicle cohort more valuable than a generic new-owner funnel

The case's incremental-investment window is 2026–2028. A newly delivered vehicle with seven included periodic events does not automatically produce customer-paid periodic servicing inside that window. In the repo's transparent time-only illustration, the first event is at month 1 and later events at six-month multiples, putting event 8 at month 42. A January 2026 delivery reaches that point in July 2029. Mileage, noncovered work and programme reimbursements can change the timing and payer, so this is a calendar boundary rather than a prediction of every owner's behavior.

The resulting commercial choice is clear: do not size near-term paid-service recovery by multiplying new xEV sales by a generic annual visit count. Begin with the installed cohort's vehicle, age, mileage, due job, programme and payer. Older petrol Toyotas may provide a separately attractive paid-job recovery pool, especially where economical channels are relevant, but they must not replace the xEV-specific answer required by the case. HEV periodic work, BEV diagnostics and customer-paid wear items require separate ledgers.

This changes targeting and measurement. The trigger is an eligible work opportunity or a protection transition, rather than merely a recent sale or app registration. An owner still under a service-linked warranty may have a valid customer-paid job. 'Out of warranty' is therefore too crude a segmentation rule. T-CARE, Lite, XTRA, prepaid benefits, warranty repairs and discretionary paid work must remain distinct. A zero customer bill also does not imply zero dealer reimbursement or gross profit.

## 3. A Toyota hybrid proposition and a BEV reassurance proposition should share infrastructure, not a single customer story

The acquired external market table reports 30,110 Toyota hybrid wholesales in 2025, including 23,934 Zenix hybrids. Zenix therefore represents79.49% of that Toyota hybrid total, and the reported top three hybrid models represent97.03%. These are external brand-flow figures, not Auto2000's installed customer base. Nevertheless, they decisively reject treating every electrified customer as a homogeneous BEV shopper concerned with charger discovery.

For the hybrid pathway, the dealer can connect an existing engine-maintenance relationship with model-specific benefits, eligible health checks and eventual appraisal evidence. For the BEV pathway, the consequential acquisition questions include household charging access, real travel pattern, affordability, model fit and confirmed demonstration availability. The same identity and case-state infrastructure can support both. The proposed customer advice, due jobs and commercial endpoint should differ.

The narrative evidence strengthens the segmentation logic. Low mileage can change the economic case for a vehicle; an existing household ICE car can make a BEV more usable; household agreement and an adviser's ability to answer concerns can affect the shortlist. These are observed reported decision mechanisms, not demographic personas invented from age or income. The useful briefing question is 'what will this car replace and what travel must it cover?' rather than 'how positive is the customer about EVs?'

## 4. Household liquidity can block a sale even when ownership information is excellent

The observed hybrid simulator already exposes a financially material timing choice. With the same Zenix variant, price, base DP, location and first-instalment convention, financing insurance reduces displayed cash upfront from Rp154,847,741 to Rp109,533,402. It increases the displayed monthly amount from Rp9,271,161 to Rp10,403,402 across 59 remaining payments. The Rp45,314,339 initial-cash difference is not solved by a better annual fuel-savings story.

For a household with an initial-cash constraint, the decision brief must show cash at delivery, monthly commitments and the status of the lender's decision. Base DP is not total initial cash. The higher ongoing commitment is also real in the illustration; financing insurance is not automatically the better option. These observations justify a cash-flow comparison using the existing simulator, rather than another calculator whose novelty rests on claiming the current one is absent.

The external mechanism studies provide a useful discipline. Petrov, Carroll and Denny's2025 study separates hypothetical choices from actual showroom behavior: monetary information changed stated choice, while the showroom trial did not detect an overall drivetrain-choice effect. Personalized information remains a plausible aid to a feasible decision. It should not be presented as a substitute for product fit, stock, price or credit acceptance. Measure actual qualified progression and delivery alongside comprehension.

## 5. Service trust attaches to the workshop relationship, so an app-only loyalty strategy can miss the retention asset

The verified Deloitte service tables distinguish a customer's vehicle brand, selling dealer and usual servicing dealer. In Q22,46% name the usual servicing dealership as the strongest relationship,24% the vehicle brand and 20% the purchase dealership. These are rounded responses from the question's general Indonesian service cohort, not Auto2000 retention percentages. They still identify why purchase loyalty, brand loyalty and workshop capture should not be treated as the same outcome.

Provider-choice Q17 gives work quality23%, trust21%, warranty18%, location/hours12% and cost7%. In a different improvement question, price/work transparency is32% and customer treatment28%. The argument is not that price or booking never matters. It is that an interface-led strategy needs to earn the quality and reliability promise of the branch that executes the work. A cheaper oil route may win one job while a dependable diagnostic route wins another.

Auto2000 should preserve the productive adviser/workshop relationship when it coordinates a customer digitally. Suppressing WhatsApp or assisted routes to inflate Digiroom attribution can destroy information or make a successful infrequent task harder. The right digital intervention carries the relevant context into the channel that can complete the work. All-channel completion distinguishes genuine recovery from an existing booking moved between interfaces.

## 6. A battery certificate is most useful when it changes the next actor's decision

Toyota already exposes eligible Hybrid Health Check and digital certificate retrieval; the current trade-in selector also includes bZ4X. A second certificate screen is therefore not a sufficient strategic distinction. The replacement opportunity is to make relevant, consented evidence usable in an actual inspection and net-upgrade offer: who accepts it, how old it can be, what it establishes, what still requires inspection and when an offer becomes executable.

The transaction object should be the net upgrade: inspected trade-in amount, new-car price/discount, outstanding finance where relevant, additional customer cash, ongoing finance obligations and timing. A customer may accept the condition evidence yet reject the market price or the next vehicle. This is why counting certificates created or valuations requested is weaker than counting inspected offers, accepted purchases and incremental replacement contribution.

Keep the five validity links explicit: technically valid measurement, understandable presentation, credible source, acceptance by the decision owner and a changed transaction. The auxiliary Audi telemetry supports a data pipeline and signal audit, not Toyota health validation. Its confidential reference-capacity series is absent. A gap-unaware integral over one drive bundle overstates its absolute terminal-energy integral by 47.52%; that shows the risk of an analytical shortcut, not a production Toyota defect. NIST usefulness responses and a proposed valuation instrument likewise cannot establish an actual Indonesian certificate premium.

## 7. Do not buy a residual-value liability to compensate for an unfinished appraisal process

A certificate, a customer-paid insurance policy, a capped dealer top-up, a full price floor and an insurer's residual-value contract are different arrangements. Their presence in the same ecosystem does not merge counterparties or transfer obligations. A motor policy for specified insured events does not become protection against ordinary market-price decline because both mention an EV battery.

The repo's contract illustration makes the choice concrete. With a Rp200m target, Rp160m inspected offer and Rp10m capped top-up, customer credit is Rp170m. A full floor instead creates a Rp40m shortfall. An assumed Rp15m insurer layer leaves Rp25m with the dealer in that illustration. These are constructed payoff states, not quotes or actuarial estimates. The exact point is legal-economic: limiting insurer recovery does not cap a separate dealer promise.

The recommended sequence is executable appraisal and transparent net-upgrade economics first. A narrowly capped, funded incentive can then be compared with an ordinary acquisition discount on incremental contribution. A long-dated floor belongs in a separate funded risk decision, with correlated exercise and loss severity, contract availability, post2028 obligations and retained exposure. A speculative residual-value-insurance partnership is not a launch dependency for the evidence-and-appraisal pathway.

**Evidence:** legacy `workstreams/friend_insurance/claim_matrix.json` INS-017–INS-023; `illustrative_risk_scenarios.csv`; `workstreams/extension_economics/constructed_joint_stress_states.csv`, `constructed_contract_tail.csv`; `reports/NUMERICAL_QA.md` NQ39–NQ41,NQ76–NQ80. Existing EV syariah motor insurance is documented; an applicable local residual-value contract is not established in the source set.

## 8. The geography needs a dealer profit perimeter as well as a travel-time perimeter

The workshop study's job-specific channels can create a better customer route without automatically creating the same profit for each entity in the Toyota/Astra ecosystem. Toyota publishes economical repair and parts channels; the new geographic package separately labels selected OtoXpert operators. This is precisely why routing should be analyzed at the job and operator level. A customer retained somewhere in the ecosystem is not identical to a completed Auto2000 branch job.

The integrated economic table should record the fulfilling entity, recognized service/parts revenue, referral or transfer arrangement if actually present, direct cost and any displaced work. The customer benefit of shorter travel can remain real even where the dealer GP treatment needs a separate scenario. Do not impose an invented internal transfer price to make the spreadsheet complete. Compare alternative perimeter assumptions visibly and preserve a useful recommendation at the operational level.

This also changes what constitutes a successful geographic intervention. A corrected destination can remove a modeled gap before any new branch, pickup subsidy or infrastructure is required. A nearer economical route can be appropriate for oil while a specialist route remains necessary for diagnosis or brake work. Use the latest geographic analysis to name these choices and preserve its origin model, comparison set, snap exclusions and boundary sensitivities. Origin cells are not customers or demand estimates.

**Evidence:** legacy `workstreams/extension_stakeholders/evidence_register.json` EXT-E01 onward; sources ES01–ES03 on G-Fleet/economical parts and repair; latest geography report and operator-labelled endpoint tables elsewhere in this release. ## 9. Better measurement can remove false strategic priorities before a new product is built

The old review corpus provides a sharp demonstration. In matched BYD listing selections,19/20 Indonesian-language records have five stars, whereas only3/20 English-language records have four or five stars. The two selections have entirely different IDs; repeated retrieval reproduces their own selections. The conclusion is source-selection sensitivity, not that one population is satisfied and another is dissatisfied. Similar date and task differences occur across apps.

Of 50 Digiroom records,37 predate the 6December2025 relaunch and 13 are on or after it. Only six of those13 describe a specific reported app interaction, and none is an independently verified transaction outcome. A broad current-defect diagnosis based on the full50 would therefore exaggerate the evidence. Positive ratings can describe a branch, vehicle, prospect experience or generic praise. A failed app attempt can still be a valid app interaction without a completed job.

The new source integration must apply the same discipline to X, YouTube and Google Maps. Requested queries are not returned content. News, promotional supply and conversation replies are not independent owner demand. A lowest-ranked review run is diagnostic case discovery, not a probability sample for complaint prevalence. Store all run membership after deduplication and keep each platform's sampling mechanism. The strategic benefit is practical: it prevents a large but misleading corpus from driving an expensive solution.

**Evidence:** legacy `workstreams/reviews/reviews_derived.csv`, `locale_recheck.json`; `workstreams/extension_reviews/EXTENSION_REVIEW_REPORT.md` Tests1–4 and the matching tables listed in the source map.

## 10. The most persuasive investment case starts with contribution thresholds, not invented conversion forecasts

The supplied FY2025 case allocates Rp69,000bn revenue and Rp6,750bn GP. Vehicle sales generate Rp3,600bn GP; after-sales collectively generates Rp3,150bn from Rp9,000bn revenue. After-sales therefore accounts for 46.7% of GP from 13.0% of revenue. Services'55% line margin is not a verified per-job, xEV or combined after-sales margin. Vehicle sales remain the largest single GP line, so an after-sales priority should not erase acquisition from the case answer.

Use a consistent bridge: incremental completed eligible work times recognized contribution, compared with the all-channel counterfactual, less incremental below-GP costs. For replacement within Auto2000, compare the margin difference and changed transaction timing, not an automatically additional whole vehicle. For channel migration, unchanged completed jobs and prices/direct costs yield zero additional transaction GP. Cost savings must be realizable rather than a count of clicks avoided.

The Rp150bn investment cap is a ceiling, not evidence that the project should spend Rp150bn. A staged commitment can begin with correction, coordination and bounded offers, then expand only if completed contribution supports it. Where customer volumes or unit economics are absent, show the break-even requirement and sensitivity rather than a point estimate labeled forecast. A recommendation can be decisive about sequencing while remaining explicit about its return threshold.

## What the mentoring notes require of the final report

The supplied eight-page mentoring note uses another case as its teaching example. Its housing/student claims are not Auto2000 evidence. The transferable tests are concrete: name the business problem, separate symptoms from causes and solutions, make the insight specific to the client/customer/decision, ask whether the opposite is plausible, and connect every recommended intervention directly to a diagnosed friction.

Applied here, 'customers want convenient service' fails because its opposite is meaningless. 'Medan should prioritize offer and confirmation over a universal pickup subsidy when the relevant oil-network access comparison is already near parity' is contestable and decision-changing, provided the latest geographic table supports the exact comparison. Likewise, 'battery reassurance matters' is generic; 'use the existing health evidence to support an executable appraisal before underwriting a future market-price floor' specifies an actor, dependency, alternative and financial consequence.

Headings in the final report should therefore state decisions and findings. The appendix can carry the model definitions, sampling limits, contradictory evidence, coordinate scenarios, source tables and code. Confidence comes from exact scope, reproducible contrasts and a chosen course of action. It does not require converting public testimony into verified outcomes or suppressing a source conflict.
