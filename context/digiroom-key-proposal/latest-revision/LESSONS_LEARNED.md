# Lessons from developing and revising this competition proposal

These are author feedback and production lessons, not claimed judge feedback or competition outcomes.

1. Revise the requested concerns while preserving approved work. Broad compression or visual replacement can remove the author's preferred reasoning and design.
2. Build to the actual page allowance. The rule is a maximum of 15 pages excluding cover, references and appendix. Count contents conservatively; use space for explanation rather than padding or smaller type.
3. Preserve causal structure in an issue tree. The business problem must visibly branch through buying, service and upgrade to subproblems and causes. A matrix can support the appendix but does not replace that diagram.
4. Restore substantive prose, not generic verbosity. Explain customer action, accountable branch action, handoff, commercial mechanism and outcome. Approximately five–six rendered lines can guide body paragraph development, but captions and purposeful short transitions need not be padded.
5. Sell the proposed solution in the main narrative; distinguish demonstrated local records from proposed production integrations in technical support. Do not imply a screenshot proves deployment.
6. Link evidence beside the precise claim. Preserve source/date/denominator and separate interview reports, public accounts, modeled origins and transaction outcomes.
7. Show the actual decision in a screenshot. Crop irrelevant browser chrome and empty canvas; keep full interface captures at appendix depth.
8. Use consistent hierarchy: solution, Overview / How It Works / Why This Works / Expected Impact, then workflow steps. Subordinate steps must not visually outrank their parent.
9. Treat font, spacing, margins and indentation as production requirements. Restored prose inherited different defaults in one pass; explicitly resetting single spacing and first-line indentation recovered space without cutting content.
10. Audit all charts and figures, including unchanged appendices, at readable size. The cash-flow label near the lower boundary and old tree headers needed correction. No-overlap checks also cover axes, legends, captions and page breaks.
11. Use a fresh critique agent in every revision cycle. It caught missing bookmarks, stale A40 wording, placeholders, chart annotations and an accidental REFERENCES-to-16 replacement introduced by the TOC updater. Restrict TOC edits to the actual TOC region and check headings afterward.
12. Preserve economic honesty. New app bookings are not necessarily incremental jobs; covered visits are not automatically customer-paid; a trade-in offer is not a delivered replacement. Keep downside and negative contribution visible even in persuasive prose.
13. A supplied financial scenario is not independent validation. Preserve assumptions and calculations without inventing a workbook or retrospectively endorsing conversion lift as observed.
14. Save durable checkpoints early. Workspace reset removed transient scripts and renders. Preserve final source, original comments, proof, source locators, tooling and hashes before ending a session. External Filebin links expire and are not the repository's source of truth.
