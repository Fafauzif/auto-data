# Competition examples

These are distilled examples of the preferred competition treatment. They are not claims that either solution has launched. Transfer the structure and tone to the new case. Keep the new brief's language and evidence.

## Pepsodent Sesuai Saya

**What to learn from the submission.** The idea has a name, a specific shopper, a normal retail journey, and a purchase outcome. Each pillar does a job the shopper can recognize. The mechanism is in the lead. Implementation and economics follow.

**Competition copy**

> **Pepsodent Sesuai Saya brings the right Premium variant to the next purchase.** Shoppers choose the benefit that matters to them. Sesuai Saya saves the matching variant as a card with its pack size, total price, and price per 100 grams. They take the card to their usual store or send it to the person shopping for them. When the tube runs low, they reopen the saved choice, check the current price, and decide whether to buy again. Shoppers whose current Basic variant meets their needs keep that routine.

**Backcast from the service.** The saved card requires a catalogue owner, approved product information, and a way to update prices. The digital team publishes the card and optional reminders through the brand's chosen channels. Retail operations owns product identification and the purchase path. Finance follows paid repeat purchase and contribution after any displaced Basic sale. If the document uses a modeled outcome, label it as a forecast and identify its material assumptions in the financial section.

**UI copy:** “Save my variant” · “Share shopping card” · “Check remaining toothpaste” · “Price last checked 5 Nov” when the price was actually checked. A missing price gets a useful status and next action.

## Auto2000 paid workshop recovery

**Decision from the case.** Auto2000 wins a defined job from an owner using an outside workshop by competing on the full visit. The offer brings together job fit, installed cost, availability, and the number of visits the owner will need. Oil and filter service is a different proposition from diagnosis of a recurring noise.

**Competition copy**

> **Auto2000 makes the next paid service job easy to choose and finish.** An Avanza owner selects an oil-and-filter change. Digiroom routes the job to an eligible Auto2000 or Auto2000-operated OtoXpert outlet and shows what the installed price includes. The outlet checks fitment, reserves the parts, and confirms a slot and collection window. If an adviser finds extra work, the owner receives the reason and revised price and decides before the scope changes. WhatsApp, a walk-in, and Digiroom all lead to the same work record.

**Backcast from the service.** Operations gives each participating outlet a job catalogue and authority to confirm inventory, scope, and a slot. Digital carries the accepted job and any later authorizations to the customer and the outlet. Launch coverage follows job-specific access; Bandung is a candidate for an access-led first wave based on the modeled residual gaps. Finance tracks completed customer-paid jobs from owners whose prior comparable job was outside Auto2000, along with recognized contribution. App-channel changes and already expected work stay outside that win-back count.

**Evidence phrasing.** “Allowing eligible Auto2000-operated OtoXpert destinations reduces modeled oil-access gaps above five minutes from 39 to 20 origins.” The figures describe modeled origins. They support a routing decision and do not establish how many owners will switch. A review about a missed complaint identifies a failure mode, not its prevalence.

**UI copy:** “What’s included” · “Choose a workshop” · “Awaiting workshop confirmation” · “Review additional work” · “Ready for collection.”

## Replace the failure at its source

| Habit to catch | What a judge or customer gets instead |
|---|---|
| “We should pilot a tool to see whether people want a clearer estimate.” | “The owner sees the installed price and what it includes before choosing a workshop.” |
| “This is just an illustrative concept for buying again.” | “The shopper reopens the saved variant, checks the price, and buys through their usual retailer.” |
| “Subject to further validation, the solution could improve retention.” | “The service keeps the same product or vehicle record through the next paid purchase. The forecast uses the stated repeat-purchase assumptions.” |
| “Phase 1: Validate the concept.” | “First release: publish the eligible catalogue, assign confirmation owners, and make the complete customer journey available in the launch locations.” |
| “Workshop booking confirmed” after a form submission. | “Request received. The workshop will confirm your slot.” |
| “Validation required” badge on a demo screen. | A useful product state, such as “Price unavailable” or “Awaiting branch confirmation,” where that is the state the customer needs. |
| “Prototype sementara, masih perlu diuji.” | “Pelanggan melihat rincian biaya dan menyetujui pekerjaan tambahan sebelum pengerjaan dimulai.” |
| “This generated Rp2 billion” with no observed outcome. | “The base case projects Rp2 billion at the stated eligible volume and contribution per completed transaction.” |
