Auto2000 sells Toyota vehicles and supports them through service, spare parts, and body and paint. Digiroom brings purchase, test-drive, service booking, trade-in, loyalty, and tracking into the same digital channel. Customers also deal directly with branches by phone, WhatsApp, and in person. That combination gives Auto2000 a way to stay involved from the first purchase decision through the next one (International Business Solution Competition [IBSC], 2026, pp. 2, 4, 11).
Indonesia’s xEV share of new-car wholesales rose from 11.9% in 2024 to 21.8% in 2025. Toyota held 51% of hybrid sales, while battery electric vehicles accounted for 59.3% of national xEV wholesales. Toyota’s hybrid position gives Auto2000 a base to build on, while the different needs of plug-in and battery electric customers make a single ownership journey a poor fit. In the case accounts, aftersales contributes 46.7% of gross profit across powertrains (IBSC, 2026, pp. 3, 8, 10). [A1]
