## Geography overlay recipe

1. **Current model.** Read [raw recomputed origins](../../analysis/geography/tables/raw_recomputed_origins.csv), [market counts](../../analysis/geography/tables/raw_recomputed_markets.csv), and [branch portfolio](../../analysis/geography/tables/branch_portfolio.csv). Use oil_gap_min for the dealer-only mode and owned_gap_min for the eligible operated-network mode.
2. **Coordinates.** Join by the pair **market + origin_id** to the [baseline origin coordinate table](../../archive/session_work_products/analysis_followup/geographic_source/Auto2000_Geographic_Study/workstreams/routing/output/origins.csv). Keep all reviewed origins and fail a reconciliation if a reviewed point cannot be placed. Check provenanced overrides in [corrected endpoints](../../sources/geography_latest/Auto2000_Lanjutan/work/coordinates/corrected_endpoints.csv). Branch and provider source tables are [official branches](../../archive/session_work_products/analysis_followup/geographic_source/Auto2000_Geographic_Study/workstreams/branches/official_branches_unique.csv), [OtoXpert inventory](../../archive/session_work_products/analysis_followup/geographic_source/Auto2000_Geographic_Study/workstreams/context/otoxpert_official_inventory.csv), and [provider inventory](../../archive/session_work_products/analysis_followup/geographic_source/Auto2000_Geographic_Study/workstreams/providers/provider_inventory.csv).
3. **Derive the display from data.** Filter strictly **greater than five minutes** against the collected oil comparator. The retained samples are Bandung 78, Surabaya 59, Medan 78, and Palembang 69. The before/after counts are respectively 17/14, 10/6, 3/0, and 9/0. The total is 39/20 over 284 paired origins. Eight residual points are assigned to Soekarno-Hatta. Use the reviewed corrected labels. Preserve nulls and ties when exposing route or branch details.
4. **Use real, licensed geometry.** Preserve source and date in the generated layer manifest. Use a documented local fallback basemap or permitted packaged geography and attribute it. Existing route traces can be used only where the records and usage rights support that. Point markers and timing comparisons cover other locations without implying real route shapes. Keep service eligibility, demonstrator and workshop availability, job quote, and purchase financing as separate operational or sample fields.
5. **Verification.** Compare the resulting scenario totals and per-market denominators to the canonical model and keep the same overlays and counts with live tiles blocked. Base analysis reproduction instructions are in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) and the [geography report](../../analysis/geography/GEOGRAPHY_INTEGRATION_REPORT.md). Overlay the KEY note separately from the base release byte-integrity check.
