# Digiroom KEY
*Know your fit. Book your service. Find your next Toyota.*

Digiroom KEY turns three decisions into transactions Auto2000 can complete. A shopper recreates their own routine in Life Test, prices their driving week, and takes the same checklist on a standard or 48-hour drive before receiving an adviser quote. An owner who uses an independent garage gets a specific workshop offer with a confirmed slot, scope, and price. A returning customer sees how the inspected value of their current car turns into a deal on their next xEV.

## The business problem and issue tree

Auto2000 can lose the next transaction even when the customer likes Toyota or already uses an Auto2000 branch. The barriers change with the decision the customer is making.

| Branch of the main problem | Customer problems | Commercial finish line |
|---|---|---|
| **K — Know Your Fit** | The household cannot picture the vehicle in daily use, the buyer cannot judge the cost, or the household still cannot tell whether the car works in real life. | A shopper **new to Auto2000** takes delivery of an xEV. |
| **E — Easy Service** | The owner prefers a specialist for the job, the visit takes too much time or effort, or the owner rejects the quoted scope or price. | An owner completes and pays for work they would otherwise take outside Auto2000. |
| **Y — Your Next Toyota** | The old car has a price range but no firm buyer offer, the cost to upgrade is unclear, or the deal breaks down between reservation and handover. | An existing Auto2000 customer takes delivery of their next xEV through Auto2000. |

The tree classifies each lost transaction by its **first binding barrier**. A shopper who understands the specifications and can afford the car but still needs to try it in their own routine goes under the real-life drive. A workshop customer who can reach the outlet but rejects the repair scope goes under quote acceptance. The detailed branches below connect each problem to its underlying cause, evidence, and solution mechanism. ## K — Know Your Fit

**The family builds its own test drive.** A family considering a Zenix Hybrid enters its garage dimensions and tries its usual seating and luggage arrangement in Life Test. It saves two checks for the real car. Can the tailgate open with the garage door shut? Can someone reach the third row with the child seat fitted? Digiroom also prices the family's normal driving week. The family carries the same checklist into a branch drive or a **48-Hour Life Test**, then shares the adviser’s quote with the person buying.

*Your garage. Your family. Your roads. One Life Test.*

### Problems, root causes, and evidence

| Problem | Root cause | Evidence | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The household cannot tell if the car fits.** | Vehicle specs do not answer how the car fits the household’s actual garage, seating, luggage, routes, and charging access. | The Wuling Air EV driver’s apartment charging worked with their routine. The BYD Atto driver reported switching to Gojek on one low-charge trip and searching for a charger on another. The same question about charging can have different answers for two households. [Interviews](https://github.com/Fafauzif/auto-data/blob/main/analysis/interviews/INTERVIEW_SYNTHESIS.md) | **Recreate your routine in 3D.** Enter garage dimensions and try the household’s usual passenger and luggage setup. Save the checks that matter, such as door and tailgate clearance or third-row access. Use the 3D workspace for supported models and carry the saved checks to the real car. |
| **The buyer cannot judge what they have to pay.** | Energy cost depends on actual weekly distance and, where relevant, charging access and tariffs. Upfront cash and instalments follow separate finance terms. The driver and buyer can be different people. | A Zenix finance illustration showed about Rp45.3 million less cash upfront with a higher monthly payment. The interviews also show family members taking different roles in purchase decisions. [Research synthesis](https://github.com/Fafauzif/auto-data/blob/main/analysis/research/RESEARCH_SYNTHESIS.md) · [Interviews](https://github.com/Fafauzif/auto-data/blob/main/analysis/interviews/INTERVIEW_SYNTHESIS.md) | **Price your own driving week.** Estimate weekly energy cost from distance, fuel price, and the home/public charging mix where relevant. | **The household still cannot tell if the car works in real life.** | A showroom visit can leave everyday use unanswered. Auto2000 already provides test-drive requests and credit simulation. K connects those steps through a checklist the household builds from its own garage, passengers, and driving costs. The branch uses those same checks to prepare the visit and finish the adviser quote. ### How K runs

1. **Digiroom creates the Life Test brief.** The shopper selects the model and variant, runs the relevant checks, and saves what they want to try on the real car. For HEVs, focus on fit, driving, and fuel use. For BEVs, capture usable home or public charging and a fallback for a regular trip. Use both sets where relevant to a PHEV. Keep the source assumptions next to the weekly estimate and carry them into the shareable brief.
2. **Branch sales confirms the drive.** A named adviser receives the brief and offers a standard drive or a 48-hour slot according to buyer eligibility and demonstrator availability. Confirm the exact model and variant, or show any difference before the customer accepts. Confirm pickup and return times for take-home drives. Use the same request to offer another branch, date, or standard drive if needed.
3. **The branch fleet lead prepares the handover.** Ring-fence the demonstrator in the calendar through return and turnaround. The branch confirms driver eligibility, applicable insurance coverage and terms, usage and distance limits, and the return procedure. The adviser records handover condition, odometer, and fuel or charge level. Give the driver a branch contact and the saved household checklist at pickup.
4. **The family tries the car in its routine.** The driver or household buyer records answers to the saved checks, such as whether the doors open comfortably in their garage or how charging fits a usual trip. At return, the adviser checks the vehicle back in, talks through the results, and attaches the dated offer for the variant the family wants to buy.
5. **Sales, finance, and delivery complete the transaction.** The buyer sees cash due and monthly payments alongside the household’s driving estimate. Record the lender decision separately from the adviser quote, then track order and delivery on the same request. Capture previous Auto2000 purchase status separately from prior Toyota ownership and first-ever xEV ownership.

**Success.** The commercial outcome is incremental retail xEV deliveries and recognized vehicle gross profit from customers new to Auto2000. Track qualified requests, confirmed standard and 48-hour drives, completed drives, issued quotes, and deliveries. Read quote and delivery progression by drive format. Track demonstrator days used, late returns, and direct cost per completed 48-hour drive to see what the take-home experience costs the branch to deliver.

**Demo starting point.** The current Life Test build already has household and driving scenarios, an ownership cost planner, branch selection, a visit brief, QR sharing, and a WhatsApp draft. The 48-hour booking option, branch confirmation, pickup and return states, and dated adviser quote extend that existing experience. [Deployed Life Test](https://auto2000demo.vercel.app/)

## E — Easy Service

An Avanza owner who usually visits a neighborhood bengkel requests an oil-and-filter change on WhatsApp. Digiroom shows an eligible outlet, the installed price, what is included, and a collection window. The outlet reserves the right filter and confirms the slot. If the adviser finds additional work, the person paying sees the explanation and revised total and can approve or decline it. The owner collects the car and receives the work record and receipt.

### Problems, root causes, and evidence

| Problem | Root cause | Evidence | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The owner trusts another workshop for this job.** | Trust attaches to the work. A good dealer experience for scheduled maintenance can coexist with an established relationship with a specialist for tires or a different job. | The Wuling driver described successful routine dealer service through WhatsApp and used a separate trusted tire workshop. This is a respondent-reported split in workshop choice. The driver's precise reasons for preferring that outlet for tires are unclear. [Interviews](https://github.com/Fafauzif/auto-data/blob/main/analysis/interviews/INTERVIEW_SYNTHESIS.md) | Show why the outlet is qualified for this vehicle and job. Name the operator, parts, included scope, and any applicable workmanship warranty. Route eligible light work to Auto2000-operated OtoXpert or an Auto2000 workshop and route other work by branch capability. Independent garages are the competing providers for the customer's job. |
| **The Auto2000 visit takes too much time or effort.** | Eligible workshops are unevenly located. A missed task or unresolved fault adds another trip even when the first drive was manageable. | Adding eligible Auto2000-operated OtoXpert locations reduced the number of modeled starting points more than five minutes behind the oil-service comparison network from 39 to 20 across 284 paired modeled origins. Two separate Soekarno-Hatta reviews describe missed or unresolved work and return visits. [Geography](https://github.com/Fafauzif/auto-data/blob/main/analysis/geography/GEOGRAPHY_INTEGRATION_REPORT.md) · [Maps cases M08–M09](https://github.com/Fafauzif/auto-data/blob/main/analysis/social/maps_deep/MAPS_MECHANISM_REPORT.md) | Offer the eligible outlet by job and location. The outlet confirms fitment, parts, and collection time for predictable work. For faults, book a defined diagnostic visit and give the adviser the symptom and previous work before arrival. Offer home service where the job and location qualify. |
| **The owner rejects the quoted work or price.** | Two shops can propose different work for the same symptom. A driver can bring in the car while a family member controls extra spend. | A Kertajaya Avanza owner reported a larger replacement proposal at Auto2000 and a cheaper outside repair. The account shows different scopes, with technical equivalence unknown. At Plaju, a reviewer objected to communication and approval. The Yaris interview shows a driver using WhatsApp for a covered visit while their father retained authority over extra charges. [Maps cases M06–M07 and M14](https://github.com/Fafauzif/auto-data/blob/main/analysis/social/maps_deep/MAPS_MECHANISM_REPORT.md) · [Interviews](https://github.com/Fafauzif/auto-data/blob/main/analysis/interviews/INTERVIEW_SYNTHESIS.md) | Put diagnosis, necessary work, deferrable items, and the installed cost of appropriate options on one written estimate. Let the driver share the quote with the payer. Record scope changes and the payer's approval before added work starts. |

### How E runs

1. **Aftersales operations** maintains an outlet-by-job catalogue with operator, supported vehicles and tasks, fitted parts, technician capability, hours, home-service coverage, and booking rules. Start the paid win-back offer with a defined oil-and-filter job. Add broader jobs using the same capability rules. Eligible hybrid and other xEV work uses its vehicle-specific coverage and suitably equipped endpoints.
2. **Digiroom or assisted intake** opens one record from the app, WhatsApp, phone, or a walk-in. Capture vehicle, job or symptom, location, original provider for comparable work, and the payer or entitlement for the current job. Show included parts, labor, tax, and exclusions for a routine offer. For repair uncertainty, quote the diagnostic scope first and issue a repair estimate after inspection.
3. **The outlet adviser** confirms fitment, parts, and a time the shop can complete. The adviser owns check-in and makes the agreed scope visible to the technician. If the work cannot proceed as agreed, the owner gets a revised time, a revised estimate, or the option to rebook.
4. **The authorized payer** accepts the initial scope and receives any change with the reason, revised total, and timing. The adviser records which version they approved. If they decline, record the declined item and the next appropriate step.
5. **Workshop and finance** record work completed, payment or reimbursement, and any unresolved symptom. A service record follows the vehicle, with the receipt and a clear next action if further work is needed.

**Success.** Measure additional completed customer-paid work and recognized Auto2000 contribution across channels. Track wins from owners who last used an independent garage for comparable work, and compare volume against ordinary practice to estimate incrementality. Record covered and reimbursement-funded jobs separately. Track confirmed visits, approved scope, paid completion, and return visits for unresolved work. A paid arrival already heading to Auto2000 is a retention opportunity with its own count. ## Y — Your Next Toyota

A Yaris Cross Hybrid owner selects a replacement xEV and books an inspection. The appraiser sends a written buy offer. Digiroom puts the inspected amount, official old-loan payoff, fees, and the replacement quote in one shareable deal. The owner sees what their existing vehicle contributes, what cash is due, and what they would pay per month. When they accept, the adviser coordinates the replacement unit, old-loan settlement, and both handovers.

### Problems, root causes, and evidence

| Problem | Root cause | Evidence | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The owner lacks a trade-in value they can act on.** | Market ranges and calculator inputs are estimates. A buyer needs to accept vehicle condition and put a firm amount and expiry in writing. | The Yaris driver checked marketplace and dealer prices and reached a range. Kertajaya reviews mention appraisal and comparison features. The uncovered customer step is moving from a range to a buy offer they can accept. [Interviews](https://github.com/Fafauzif/auto-data/blob/main/analysis/interviews/INTERVIEW_SYNTHESIS.md) · [Maps cases M19–M20](https://github.com/Fafauzif/auto-data/blob/main/analysis/social/maps_deep/MAPS_MECHANISM_REPORT.md) | Book an inspection. With permission, offer relevant service history or hybrid health evidence to the appraiser. Show which evidence they accept and issue a written buy offer with the buyer, conditions, and validity. |
| **The owner cannot tell what the upgrade will cost.** | The gross trade-in figure, old-loan payoff, transaction fees, new-car price, and financing terms sit in separate decisions. | The team's trade-in flow separates trade-in value, loan settlement, fees, net equity, cash due, instalments, and any balloon payment. That distinction turns an estimate of what the old car is worth into what the owner can actually use. [Team's Goal 3 flowchart](https://chatgpt.com/library?file_id=libfile_49eee12845e0819188b78c57956dec97&account_id=personal) | Calculate **net equity = inspected buy offer − official loan payoff − applicable trade-in fees**. Show available credit or the shortfall alongside the exact replacement offer, cash due, monthly payment, term, and any balloon payment. |
| **The deal can fall apart before handover.** | The appraisal, finance decision, deposit, and inventory hold can run on separate timelines. | One review reported difficulty getting a deposit returned after a trade-in stalled. Another reported a vehicle going to another buyer despite a booking fee and credit approval. These reports identify failure points in coordinating a deal. [Maps cases M21–M22](https://github.com/Fafauzif/auto-data/blob/main/analysis/social/maps_deep/MAPS_MECHANISM_REPORT.md) | Give one adviser ownership of the deal. Align offer expiry, lender conditions, and the reservation for the exact replacement unit. Show payment and refund terms before accepting money, then reconcile the old car, old loan, and replacement delivery in the same record. |

### How Y runs

1. **Sales** opens the upgrade record when a returning customer starts shopping and selects the replacement model. Direct repeat purchases can proceed through a vehicle quote. For a trade-in, the owner books an inspection and chooses which vehicle evidence to share.
2. **The appraiser** records inspection results and issues the buy offer. **The current lender** supplies the official payoff and its validity. **The adviser** applies the agreed fees, calculates net equity, and puts the replacement quote and proposed financing alongside it.
3. **Inventory and the finance provider** confirm an exact unit and the relevant funding conditions. The adviser tracks both dates and dependencies. If an offer expires, financing changes, or a unit becomes unavailable, provide an updated deal or a clear option to pause.
4. **The owner** accepts the complete deal and the disclosed deposit terms. **The adviser and settlement team** coordinate custody of the trade-in, lender payoff, customer credit or shortfall, and replacement handover. Record completed delivery and reconcile any deposit, fee, or refund.

**Success.** Measure incremental repeat Auto2000 xEV deliveries and recognized vehicle gross profit. Track inspection attendance, firm offers issued, offers accepted, reservations confirmed, settlements completed, and delivered replacements. Record whether trade-in was part of the deal and monitor refunds or expired deals so the team can see where sales break down.

**Commercial decision.** The inspection-backed upgrade offer is the core of Y. A guaranteed resale floor addresses a separate future price risk. The Atto interview captured a preference for certainty, while the Yaris interview captured actual price research that reached a range. A floor belongs in a separate commercial decision with a named promisor, terms, and funding for the difference. The team's flowchart leaves residual-value insurance unconfirmed. [Interview synthesis](https://github.com/Fafauzif/auto-data/blob/main/analysis/interviews/INTERVIEW_SYNTHESIS.md)

## How the three pillars work together

| Shared capability | Operating rule |
|---|---|
| **One customer-visible record per transaction** | Carry the vehicle, branch, adviser, status, quote version, and next action across channels. Let the customer reopen a drive request, service job, or upgrade deal. Give the responsible team the same approved information. |
| **A named person at every handoff** | Sales owns the demonstrator and vehicle offer. Aftersales owns job eligibility, parts, and work completion. The upgrade adviser coordinates the appraiser, lender, inventory, and settlement. Each actor confirms the state they control. |
| **Consent and access that fit the task** | Send a shareable view to the household buyer or payer with their authorization. Share only the needed vehicle or quote details. Give family links expiry or revocation controls and record who approved a scope change. |
| **Completion across channels** | Use the same identifiers for app, WhatsApp, phone, and assisted interactions. Count the work order or delivery once. Record acquisition and repeat delivery as distinct outcomes. Attribute workshop contribution to the fulfilling Auto2000 operator and track covered work by payer. |
| **Coordination across ownership decisions** | A completed E job can supply relevant service evidence to Y with permission. A customer facing a repair-versus-replacement decision can choose either path. Attribute the completed outcome to the path they actually choose. |

### Delivery sequence

1. **Release K from the existing Life Test experience.** Digiroom connects the household checks and weekly cost estimate to branch scheduling and a dated quote. Branch sales offers the 48-Hour Life Test where eligible buyers, demonstrators, and handover arrangements are available, alongside the standard drive.
2. **Release E with the outlet job catalogue and a completed paid-service journey.** Aftersales confirms eligible facilities, job scope, parts, price, workshop capacity, and who owns approvals. Route Medan and Palembang through their eligible existing network for oil work. Use the remaining Bandung gaps to guide where visit preparation and eligible home service matter most.
3. **Release Y with the inspected offer and coordinated reservation.** Sales and settlement agree on who accepts the appraisal, obtains the lender payoff, confirms the replacement unit, and reconciles both handovers.
4. **Connect the records and review commercial results.** Digital owns status visibility, branch teams own fulfillment, and finance reconciles recognized contribution across channels. The case's IDR 150 billion ceiling for 2026–2028 governs total investment. Each release has its own operating cost and incremental contribution.
